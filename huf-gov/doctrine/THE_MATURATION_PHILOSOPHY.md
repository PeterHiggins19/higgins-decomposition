## The stance

> **The opposite of publish‑or‑perish — and of publish‑and‑perish.** The preference is to **mature, publish, and
> mature some more.** If the work can be advanced alone, there is no need to involve others until more help is
> genuinely needed. There is **no past publication to defend, no history to protect** — so every document and
> every paper is **open to revision, always.** Prior "approved / locked" states are **stable checkpoints, not
> freezes.** The whole chain runs the collective review **again and again**, without fear of development churn.

This is why **all locks are lifted** (2026‑06‑25): P1 and every paper are open to fine adjustment. A *single word
in the right place* can be the difference between understanding and ignorance for one reader — so small, subtle
edits are first‑class work, not noise.

## Why this is the right discipline here

- **Maturity beats speed.** A result held and re‑tested until it is genuinely solid is worth more than a fast
  result defended forever. Re‑opening is strength, not weakness.
- **Self‑advance first, involve when needed.** Casting the *biggest, most explanatory web of involvement* is the
  goal — but only when the work is ready to reward the involvement. Until then, advance it directly.
- **No sunk cost.** With no history to protect, the only loyalty is to *being right and clear* — which is exactly
  what lets the chain keep improving.

So far, on the order of **~151 people out of ~8 billion** have any sense of the usefulness here. The aim is to
shed light on the **blind side of all observations** so the world can make **better decisions** — and the rise of
intelligent systems makes broad adoption a real possibility, since *anyone doing math could be made aware* of the
simpler, exact, deterministic route (see [`../../library/MATH_Hs_MAKES_SIMPLER.md`](../../library/MATH_Hs_MAKES_SIMPLER.md)).

> **The scale figure, fenced (Tier 3).** An early, **unmeasured** estimate put the world‑economy value of
> **ratio‑blindness** — what is missed because readings see totals and not proportions — into the **trillions**, *if*
> broad adoption occurred and roughly **a quarter of all observations** were suddenly made visible. This is a
> **vision figure to convey scale and motivate the web of involvement — not a measurement, not a forecast.** The
> honest, durable claim is the small one: for a real class of everyday math, the compositional route is simpler,
> exact, and deterministic, and that visibility compounds as intelligent systems spread.

## How it changes day‑to‑day work

1. **Everything is open to revision.** No document is frozen; improve any of it, however small.
2. **Fine adjustments are real work.** One precise word > one more paragraph. Sharpen for the single confused
   reader.
3. **Re‑run the collective review, repeatedly.** The chain is meant to be reviewed again and again; that is the
   maturation, not a sign of trouble.
4. **Keep the honest tiers.** Maturing never means inflating — claims stay tiered, the vision figure stays fenced.
5. **Peter executes every outward step.** Maturation is internal and free; publication remains Peter's gate.
