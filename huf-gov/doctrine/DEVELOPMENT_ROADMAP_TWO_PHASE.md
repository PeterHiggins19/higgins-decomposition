## Phase 2 — "those who benefit the larger industry as a whole"

The beneficiary criterion is deliberately broad and public‑minded, not a private hand‑off:

- **Broad public/industry benefit, not narrow private capture** — the tuned versions go where they lift the
  *whole field*, not a single rent‑seeker.
- **Through a legitimate steward** — a national government, a standards body, or a public‑good institution (the
  Canada steward path is the worked example).
- **Export rules respected** — sensitive tuned how‑to is deferred upward to the steward's authority, never
  distributed on our own.
- **Favouring the trust‑and‑quality layer** — whoever advances the field's auditable, deterministic quality
  standard, where a small early mover gains influence beyond its volume (the momentum model, `87fbaaa3`).

## The bridge — why Phase 1 makes Phase 2 work (not redundant)

Publishing the science **first** is not giving away the advantage — it is *building* it:

- The published, **verifiable** principle earns trust that no pitch can; it starts the diffusion lead.
- The fab/standard advantage was never the published method (~96% copy‑resistant, `e03a7465`) — so Phase 1 costs
  nothing of the moat.
- Phase 2's tuned versions then land into a field that **already understands and trusts the principle**, and the
  early‑mover/standard‑layer logic means the prepared steward's head start **compounds** — without ever turning
  the tuned how‑to into a free‑for‑all.

So the order is the honest one: **give the science to everyone; offer the refined application, later, to whoever
will use it for the broad good.**
