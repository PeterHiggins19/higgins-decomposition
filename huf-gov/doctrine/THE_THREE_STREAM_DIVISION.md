## 1. Why divide, and why backwards

The science‑learning order is **math → method → application → value**: you teach the mathematics first, then what
it does, then who pays. That order is right for *learning*. It is the wrong order for *the road* — for getting a
real system adopted. The **on‑road problem** is solved by going **backwards**:

```
   $$$$  →  WHERE  →  WHY  →  HOW  →  POLITICS  →  MATH
   value    place      need    method   governance   the base
  (dollars) (which     (the    (inject- (export /   (the principle,
            industry/  gap     ion, the  steward,    last to state,
            jurisdic-  Hs      pilot,    Canada)     first in
            tion)      fills)  conformance)          foundation)
```

- **Dollars first.** Where is the worth? (The value envelope, the yield/excursion lever.) Money names the entry.
- **Where.** Which jurisdiction and industry can actually use it? (Canada; packaging/test/photonics; the EUV
  domain.)
- **Why.** The need the read fills (the silent drift, the two‑sided cliff, the value gap).
- **How.** The method of injection (read‑only pilot → edge node → gated assist; the conformance suite).
- **Politics.** The governance — export sensitivity, the steward, the defer‑upward.
- **Math, last.** The principle is the *base*: stated last on the road because it is *assumed* underneath
  everything above it. Last to present, first in foundation.

**Cross‑flow, not one‑way.** Science and politics flow *across* each other — the same sense‑up / control‑down
cross‑flow as the distributed robotic model. The science feeds the *why* and the *value*; the politics gates the
*how* and the *where*. Neither leads alone; the dollar opens the door and the math holds the floor.

> The science‑learning order builds understanding. The on‑road order builds adoption. We keep both, and we use
> the right one for the right reader.
