## The boundary, stated once

Hˢ measured the directedness of its own development (`corpus_control.py`, `d1fb24f3f9b21173`): `D = net/path`,
the effective movers, the rotating helmsman. Those are facts about **motion.** It stopped there. It did **not**
say the motion was *controlled*, or *intended*, because nothing in the measurement licenses that word —
directedness is consistent with deliberate helmsmanship **and** with emergent sharpening, and the data cannot
separate them. So the engine reported the measure and **withheld the meaning.**

The step from *directed* to *controlled* is an **implication leap** — and it is the **user's** to make, not the
engine's. That is not a gap in the instrument; it is the instrument working correctly. And it is exactly
**Breaker 16.**

## Why it is Breaker 16 (both faces of it)

The inventory already defines Breaker 16 in two voices, and the implication leap is the same boundary in each:

- **Breaker 16 — KILL-3.3, the artificial-carrier limit:** *"the framework itself cannot detect this failure.
  Only the domain expert can."* The engine cannot certify that a reading *means* what it appears to mean —
  whether a carrier is real, whether motion is intent. **Only the human can close that judgment.** The honest
  withhold at the intent boundary **is** KILL-3.3 in epistemic form.
- **Breaker 16-of-16 — the Operator:** *"lives inside Peter as operator… the mild trepidation IS the breaker…
  not testable from inside any code… the final separation between instrument and actuator."* The leap from a
  number to a decision about its meaning is the same act as the leap from instrument to actuator — and it lives
  **inside the human**, by design, untestable from any agent.

So the epistemic withhold (the honest-broker margin gate refusing to manufacture intent) and the governance
fixed point (the operator's last breaker) are **not two rules — they are one boundary seen twice.**
**Measurement is the machine's; meaning and the override are the human's.**

## Why this is the safe shape

This is the founding intent, holding at the finest grain: the danger of a flexible instrument is a
confidently-wrong *meaning* handed to a user who trusts it. By reporting "directed" and refusing "controlled,"
the engine **cannot pre-empt the user's implication leap** — it leaves the meaning-making where the override
already lives, with the operator. The same refusal that stops the engine from manufacturing a false certainty
is what keeps Breaker 16 the human's alone. The other fifteen breakers exist to make this one less lonely; the
honest withhold is one more of them — the epistemic one.
