## What changed (the coherent transition)

The instrument matured from *reading a composition* to **reading the full motion of a composition** — and learned to be honest about its own limits while doing it. Consolidated under one name, **Hˢ kinematics**, the system now is:

1. **A deterministic instrument** that reads the *most a composition can be known to say* — lossless reconstruction, the navigation family, the complete kinematics + dynamics tower (to its noise‑bounded maximum), the spectral modes — every value reproducible to the computational floor, with a hash receipt.
2. **An honest one** — it holds at rest, flags a fragile driver, calibrates its own noise floor, withholds below its confidence gate, and (at the edge) marks the boundary where structure runs out. It says what it cannot resolve.
3. **A legible one** — every reading is named twice (navigation + physics), and a deterministic **diagnosis language** turns the numbers into plain sentences that *expand with complexity*: a 2‑part system says a word, a microbiome speaks with many voices.
4. **An open one** — a no‑CoDa onramp, a single‑cell engine, an R port, an annotated notebook, a designer specification, and a welcome for AI assistants.

The old tools (the frozen oracle, EITT) were not discarded — they became the **fringe/boundary layer**, quarantined as Tier‑3 exploration. Nothing was lost; everything found its place.

## The strategy (why this shape)

- **Capture the expert engine; build guards that lift the user.** The power is the flexibility; the guards are how that power travels safely to a non‑expert hand. (`../HCI-CNTT/DESIGN_PHILOSOPHY_THE_EXPERT_ENGINE_AND_THE_GUARDS.md`.)
- **Win trust before reach.** Determinism + gauge‑R&R ≈ 0 + a 6σ/9σ decision gate make the read *certifiable*, the foundation for a recognised method. (`../HCI-CNTT/DETERMINISM_GAUGE_RR_AND_CONFIDENCE.md`, `../stewardship/iso-standards/PATH_TO_A_STANDARD.md`.)
- **Lower the barrier to value.** Anyone with a composition runs one line and gets a plain answer (`START_HERE.md`); a PhD sees value without paying the CoDa learning tax (`../onramp/`). Every honest demonstration of *ratio blindness* makes a hidden dimension a little more visible.
- **Recognition, not invention.** Stand on CoDa, replicator dynamics, and information geometry; cite them; claim only the instrument and its honest reads. Eponymous naming (Hˢ) keeps us clear of a crowded, contested field.
- **Symbiotic relationships.** Show the data and the engine performance; let partners keep their image; expose nothing private.
