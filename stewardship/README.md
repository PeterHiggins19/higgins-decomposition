## Why this is a separate folder

`industrial-instruments/` is the **commercial / deterministic‑instrument** line — gas and process‑fluid monitoring, paid deployments, private dossiers behind the carrier filter. This folder is the opposite posture: **public good, not commerce.** Here the instrument is offered to standards bodies and conservation science as a contribution, not a product. Same engine, same determinism, same "the instrument reads, the expert decides" — different purpose and different ethics of engagement.

Keeping the two apart is deliberate. A conservation scientist or a standards committee should never wonder whether the reading they are shown is shaped by a sale. In this folder there is nothing to sell.

## The two pursuits in one line each

- **ISO** — three monitoring questions are already standardised (magnitude, identity, trend); the fourth, **composition**, has no ISO home. MC‑4 proposes to fill that gap, with the CoDa community as the mathematical foundation. *A proposal, not an accepted standard.*
- **Ramsar** — the Convention on Wetlands (1971; 172 Parties; ~2,500 sites). Hˢ offers two tiers — multi‑site governance monitoring and per‑site field‑work compositional analysis — to the people who steward wetlands. *Interest expressed; nothing deployed.*
