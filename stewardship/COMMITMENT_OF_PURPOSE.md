## The purpose, in one paragraph

Hˢ exists to read the **internal balance of whole systems** — the proportional structure that magnitude, identity, and trend measurements miss. Much of that capability has obvious commercial use, and that lives in `industrial-instruments/`. **This folder is for the other half: the public good.** Two systems in the world are measured by *composition* and stewarded by people who answer to the public, not to a market — **international measurement standards** and **the conservation of wetlands**. To both, Hˢ offers a deterministic, auditable instrument and asks nothing back except that the work be checked. That is the commitment: *a world‑helping instrument, offered and not sold, where only the expert decides.*

## What this track commits to

1. **Serve, don't sell.** In this folder the instrument is a contribution to standards bodies and conservation science. There is no product, no fee, no dossier behind the carrier filter. The reading a wetland scientist or a standards committee sees is never shaped by a sale.
2. **The expert decides — always (open loop).** Hˢ reports composition, drift, the steering carrier, and the regime; it never recommends an action, triggers an alert, or closes the loop. The conservation scientist judges whether a drift is natural succession, climate response, or degradation. The standards committee judges what to formalise. *The instrument is the altimeter, not the hand that pushes.* (See the Open‑Loop / Skydiver Doctrine.)
3. **Name the gaps honestly.** Ramsar is a messy political‑scientific‑ecological treaty system, not a clean dataset; MC‑4 has no ISO home *yet* and is a proposal, not an accepted standard. The honest register (the Ramsar Complexity Gap; the "no ISO home" status) is part of the offer, so anyone can see exactly where the bridge is incomplete and decide whether to help build the missing spans.
4. **Determinism as a public guarantee.** Same input → same output → same hash, on any machine, a year later. For a stewardship instrument this is not a nicety: it is what lets a sceptic, a regulator, or a future scientist reproduce the reading and trust it without trusting us.
5. **Interest expressed, never acquired.** ISO/TC 69, the Ramsar Secretariat, and the CoDa community are *target audiences*, not partners. Every letter is a draft; nothing has been sent; Peter is the sole contact gate.

## Why the two systems share a folder

Standards and conservation look unrelated until you see what they have in common: both are **public commons measured by proportion**, both are currently **blind to the compositional category**, and both are stewarded for everyone rather than owned by anyone. A grid, a portfolio, an ecosystem can pass every magnitude/identity/trend check while its internal balance quietly degrades — *ratio blindness.* Closing that blind spot for a standards body protects measurement itself; closing it for a wetland protects a living system. Same instrument, same fourth category, same offer.

## The human‑and‑instrument framing (stated soberly)

The Ramsar work was first framed as a *proving ground* for human and AI working as co‑workers on "the very earth framework." Stripped to its grounded core, the idea is simple and defensible: humans and machines share one planet and depend on the same ecosystems; an instrument that helps keep those systems legible — that shows their internal balance honestly, and leaves the judgment to people — is worth building together. The fuller, more philosophical version of that argument lives in the Human‑AI Accord (a source document, not a claim); this charter keeps only the part that earns its place: **build the instrument, keep the loop open, let the experts who steward the commons decide.**

## What success looks like (claim‑tiered, none yet earned here)

- **Tier 2 (discipline, now):** the offer is honest, deterministic, open‑loop, and clearly separated from the commercial line.
- **Tier 3 (to earn):** a standards body engages MC‑4 on its merits; a wetland scientist runs Hˢ on real site data and finds the read useful; a reproduction by an independent party confirms it. None of this is claimed today — it is the pursuit.

*The instrument reads. The expert decides. The hashes carry the receipts. The loop stays open. Here, the work is offered, not sold.*
