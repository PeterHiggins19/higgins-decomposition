## The idea, plainly

Extend a person's senses with a distributed field of sensors — across space, across modalities, across
hardware generations — and feed what they gather back to the user as **perception they can trust the way they
trust their own hands.** A hand does not lie about what it touches; the requirement on a sense-extension is
the same: **whatever it delivers must be faithful, or it must not deliver.** That single requirement —
*no perversion to the touch* — is the hard part, and it is exactly the part Hˢ was built for. The sensors and
the coupling are other people's frontier; **the trust is ours.**

The seed Peter named long ago — a direct auditory-cortex connection for pure sound enjoyment — is one
instance of the general shape: a sense, extended, with nothing falsified between the world and the percept.

## What Hˢ actually supplies (the trust layer, measured)

A sense-extension is only safe to point at perception if three guarantees hold. `sensorial_fidelity.py`
demonstrates all three on a toy spectral scene (receipt `2076c054626a3b91`):

1. **No perversion under advancing hardware.** A sensor 50× more sensitive delivers the **same percept** —
   residual 2.5×10⁻¹⁶. The device's loudness/brightness/exposure does **not** alter the perceived *structure*,
   because the compositional read cancels overall gain exactly (common-mode rejection). This is why the
   apparatus **grows with future hardware** instead of being obsoleted by it: the read lives in the ratios,
   and the ratios survive the hardware swap.
2. **Faithful-or-flagged, never silently wrong.** Per-channel mis-calibration *does* move the percept — and
   the system says so honestly: it **measures the drift and flags it** (Aitchison drift 0.16 > threshold),
   and once the characterized calibration is applied the percept is **restored exactly** (residual
   2.5×10⁻¹⁶). The extension is never silently perverted; it is faithful, or it tells you it isn't. This is
   the self-reporting-drift discipline, at the stakes of perception.
3. **Honest withhold.** On a degenerate read — structure collapsed onto the noise floor — the gate **withholds
   the percept entirely** rather than manufacturing one. The cortex is never handed an invented sensation.

These three are the same trust primitives from everywhere else in the system (common-mode rejection, the
locked invariant, drift self-report, the margin gate), now stated as the contract a sense-extension must meet
before it earns the touch.

## Why the structure is right — recursion, reciprocity, budget, partition

Peter's four words name why the apparatus is coherent rather than merely ambitious:

- **Budget + partition** = closure. A sensory field is a conserved whole apportioned across its parts; reading
  the partition of the budget is reading the percept. The simplex is the natural home of a sense.
- **Reciprocity** = the user and the field extend each other: the field amplifies the user's reach, and the
  user's attention (the helmsman) steers the field. Influence runs both ways, by design — the global-user-system
  axiom, embodied in a sensorium.
- **Recursion** = *the science helps the science to self-achieve.* Hˢ reads Hˢ; the field's own readings feed
  its calibration and its trust; the feedback chain compounds. **The physics tests itself** — the triad has
  the device's own physics checked by an independent Q and certified by the trust model, so the apparatus is
  self-verifying as it grows.

That is the sense in which this is a "cool tool" that improves itself: each faithful reading is also a datum
about the instrument, so the instrument sharpens with use rather than drifting with it.
