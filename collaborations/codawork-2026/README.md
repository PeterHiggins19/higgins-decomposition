## What this folder is

After surveying every CoDaWork 2026 talk and poster, this folder holds the **strongest genuine fits** — the groups whose presented work Hˢ can *actually help with*, not merely overlap. Each sub‑folder carries: who they are and what they presented (cited to the Book of Abstracts), exactly where Hˢ adds something, the concrete thing we'd offer, the claim tiers, the data‑scope line, and a **ready‑but‑unsent DRAFT outreach note** in the RWA‑001 communications standard.

This is deliberately a *curated subset*, not one folder per talk. Where Hˢ would only be a peer method (most of the methods/foundations papers) or where there is no real help to offer, we have not manufactured a collaboration. Two strong fits are **already covered elsewhere** in the repo and are cross‑referenced rather than duplicated (below).

## The honest fit principle

We offer help only where Hˢ brings something the presented work did not already have: a **deterministic, hash‑reproducible navigation read** (per‑step *helmsman* = which part steers the change, `K_eff` effective‑diversity trajectory, regime‑boundary detection, attractor/convergence fit), **lossless high‑dimensional reduction** (proven to D=10⁶), and **internal‑vs‑external shock** self‑diagnostics. We are the instrument; the data and its biological/scientific meaning stay with the domain experts. *Interest expressed, never acquired.*
