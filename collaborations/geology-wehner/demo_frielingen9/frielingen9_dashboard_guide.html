Frielingen-9 dashboard — companion guide

What each field is, and exactly how it is computed  ·  Hs / CNT / CNQ on real mudstone
How to use this. The dashboard is an instrument: it reports how the composition moves down the core. This guide explains every number and panel and the maths behind it. It makes no geological claims — surfaces, provenance, redox, diagenesis, events are yours to read, led by the data and the displays.

1 · The data

Composition (what the instrument navigates), D = 4: [SiO2, Al2O3, Rb, Zr]. SiO2/Al2O3 are wt %; Rb/Zr are mg/kg, divided by 104 so they enter the same closure. This 4-part subcomposition embeds exactly the two ratios geologists already use here — Si/Al (grain size / quartz–clay) and Zr/Rb (sorting / heavy-mineral). D = 4 is special: it is the one size where CNQ is exact (see §3).

Independent calibration targets (NOT in the composition): CaCO3 and TOC (wt %). Held out so they can be used as an outside check on what the composition-only navigation finds.

Extent: 219 discrete samples, 15.4–249.8 m. Source: Thöle et al. 2019, core Frielingen-9 (Lower Cretaceous, Lower Saxony Basin), PANGAEA 897615, CC-BY 4.0; paper 10.1002/dep2.83. Input file: frielingen9_xrf_4part.csv.

2 · The pipeline at a glance

Every sample is a point on the simplex (parts that sum to 1). Each step down-core is the move from one sample to the next. The instrument measures that move in a geometry where ratios — not raw amounts — are what count.

raw parts ──closure──▶ x (sums to 1) ──CLR──▶ centred log-ratios (ratio geometry) ──ILR──▶ orthonormal coords (3 numbers per sample, D=4) │ per step: Aitchison step ‖Δ‖ · helmsman · power-share · activation α · K_eff · regime per step: CNQ = radial ‖ilr‖ + bearing rotation (quaternion, exact at D=4) whole core: barycenter (ILR→PCA 2-D), calibration r vs CaCO3 / TOC

Why log-ratios: compositions are closed (a rise in one part forces others down), so ordinary differences mislead. Log-ratio geometry (Aitchison) removes that artefact and makes "how much did the mixture really change" well-defined.

3 · Core computations

Closure

x_k = v_k / Σ_j v_j (parts now sum to 1)

Puts the four parts on a common total so only relative proportions remain.

CLR — centred log-ratio

clr_k = ln(x_k) − (1/D) Σ_j ln(x_j) ( = ln of x_k ÷ geometric-mean )

Each part expressed against the average part. CLR vectors live in ratio space; differences in CLR are the real compositional moves.

ILR — isometric log-ratio (Helmert / orthonormal balances)

ilr = clr · H H is D×(D−1), orthonormal column c (c = 1…D−1): balances the first c parts against part c+1, scaled by √(c/(c+1)) → unit-norm, uncorrelated axes

Three independent coordinates (D−1 = 3) with no built-in correlation, so distances and PCA behave normally. Because H is orthonormal, ‖Δilr‖ = ‖Δclr‖ — the step is the same whether measured in CLR or ILR.

Aitchison step confirmed

step_i = ‖clr_i − clr_{i−1}‖ = √( Σ_k (Δclr_k)² )

The size of the compositional move between consecutive samples — a true distance on the simplex. Big step = the mixture reorganised a lot.

Helmsman

helm_i = argmax_k |Δclr_k| (which part steers this step)

The single part whose log-ratio moved most. The coloured ticks in the log and the readout name it.

Power share

pw_k = (Δclr_k)² / Σ_j (Δclr_j)² (sums to 1 across parts)

How the step's "energy" is split among the four parts — the bars in the readout.

Activation coefficient α — the deceptive-drift detector

α_k = pw_k / ρ_k ρ_k = mass share of part k at the previous sample flag fires when: α_k ≥ 3 AND ρ_k ≥ 0.1% (the share guard)

α > 1 means a part does more structural work than its abundance. A tiny part with a large α is "deceptive drift" — it punches far above its weight. Here Rb and Zr sit at ≈0.024 % share, below the 0.1 % guard, so the formal flag stays silent (the guard avoids unstable division by near-zero); the dashboard still shows their α (often hundreds–thousands) marked with * in amber, because that punching-above-weight is exactly the signal. The bulk parts (Si/Al) are above the guard but never reach α ≥ 3, so nothing trips the flag in this core.

K_eff — effective number of active parts

K_eff = exp( −Σ_k x_k ln x_k ) (1 ≤ K_eff ≤ D)

The exponential of compositional entropy. Near D when the mixture is even; near 1 when one part dominates.

Regime tripwire

regime_i = step_i > median(step) + 2 · (1.4826 · MAD(step))

A robust (median/MAD) outlier threshold on the step series. Marks unusually large moves — candidate boundaries, for you to compare against picked surfaces.

CNQ — Compositional Navigation Quaternion (native, D = 4) confirmed (exact at D=4)

radial_i = ‖ilr_i‖ (how far from the centre) bearing_i = arccos( û_i · û_{i−1} ) in degrees, û = ilr/‖ilr‖ (turn angle)

At D = 4 the ILR vector has 3 components, which map to the imaginary part of a quaternion — so the path's magnitude and its turn between steps are read exactly, with no projection loss. (At D = 8 this is experimental experimental; above that it is a declared lossy projection not native.)

Barycenter trajectory (ILR → PCA, 2-D)

SVD of centred ilr → keep first 2 principal scores sign convention: flip each axis so sample 0 is negative (deterministic)

A flat 2-D shadow of the 3-D ILR path, just for the eye. The sign rule makes the picture reproducible rather than mirror-ambiguous.

SHOCK shading

t = min(1, step_i / (0.8 · max step)); redder + thicker segment as t → 1

Paints the barycenter path by step size so the big compositional jumps stand out visually.

Calibration r (independent check)

r = Pearson( step , |ΔCaCO3| ) and Pearson( step , |ΔTOC| )

CaCO3 and TOC are not in the composition, so a non-zero correlation means the composition-only motion partly tracks something it never saw. Here r(step,|ΔCaCO3|) ≈ +0.24 and r(step,|ΔTOC|) ≈ −0.08.

Diagnostic ratios & directness

log Si/Al = ln(SiO2/Al2O3) log Zr/Rb = ln(Zr/Rb) flips = # times helmsman changes; directness = 1 − flips/(N−2)

The two ratios are the familiar grain-size / sorting handles. Directness near 1 = one part keeps steering; near 0 = the driver changes constantly (here 0.43).

5 · Radar — CLR deviation at depth (middle, default)

A 4-spoke star (one spoke per part) of the current sample's CLR, relative to the section mean ring (dashed). Spoke out = that part is enriched vs its average; in = depleted. The zoom slider magnifies deviations around the mean so small but real moves (e.g. Al2O3) become visible.

spoke_k ∝ 0.5 + zoom · 0.42 · clr_k / max|clr| (clamped to the ring)

This is the per-depth shape of the composition in ratio space — the radar "rings" when one part pulls away from the others.

6 · Barycenter trajectory + SHOCK (middle, toggle)

The 2-D PCA shadow of the ILR path up to the current depth (gold dot = now; dark red dots = regime samples). Toggle SHOCK to colour/thicken the path by step size. It shows the route the composition has travelled, not just where it is.

7 · Calibration scatter (middle-lower)

Each point is one step: x = |ΔCaCO3| (independent), y = Aitchison step (composition-only). The printed r is the Pearson correlation; the gold point is the current step. A positive slope means bigger composition moves tend to fall where carbonate also changes — an outside corroboration, not a built-in one.

11 · Caveats & honesty

Claim tiers travel with every number. confirmed the CNT steps/helmsman/regime and the native D = 4 CNQ. experimental the calibration mapping (CaCO3 correlation modest, no picked-surface overlay yet). Nothing here is not-implemented dressed up as more.

Faithful method, not the engine binary. These scripts are a transparent reimplementation of the documented CNT/CNQ method — readable end to end — not the hash-stamped canonical engine. Running the same composition through that engine for byte-provenance is a listed next step.

D = 4 subcomposition. Only Si, Al, Rb, Zr. The companion high-resolution core-scan datasets (Ca, Ti, K, Mn, Fe, Sr… at 1 cm) would change which part is helmsman and let the deceptive-drift flag properly fire.

The instrument does not interpret. Whether the 19 tripwires are sequence boundaries, whether Zr/Rb steering is provenance or diagenesis, whether the +0.24 is dilution or coincidence — those are yours, led by the data and the displays.
