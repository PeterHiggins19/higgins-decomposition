Hs at the front end

The highest-leverage position for a compositional framework · forward look
STATUS: positioning / vision · design fit is real · the seat must still be earned by validation
Thesis. The front end — the sensor edge, the first reduction of the raw data firehose — is the highest-leverage place a framework can sit. It is also the highest trust bar, which is exactly why Hs's core properties (deterministic, tiny, reconstructable, auditable) are the entry ticket rather than nice-to-haves. The seat is available; it is not yet held.

1 · Why the front end is the leverage point

Raw sensing produces far more data than can be stored, downlinked, or interpreted. So the first reduction — what gets kept, compressed, or summarised at the source — is where the most information is irreversibly discarded, and where the terms for every downstream stage are set. Whoever defines that first transform shapes everything after it. That makes the front end the maximum-leverage chokepoint in any sensing pipeline — and, because it is usually mission- or safety-critical, the place with the highest trust bar. Highest value and hardest to earn are the same fact.

3 · The hardware path (recap)

NOW ground reprocessing of existing cubes (EMIT, CRISM) — pure software, prove value [feasible] MID fixed CNQ kernel on FPGA / VPU / NASA HPSC — onboard triage + smart-downlink [plausible] FAR adaptive autonomy: densify facets where curvature appears, steer sampling (feedforward) [vision]

The quaternion is already the native language of spacecraft attitude systems; the platforms (HPSC ~500× the RAD750; onboard VPUs flying on PhiSat-2) and the high-D compositional sensors (EMIT's 285 bands, CRISM, PIXL) exist now. The computational fit is excellent; what is unproven is the science payoff, not the feasibility of running it.

4 · Coherence with HUF

The front end is also where the carrier-filter reduction naturally belongs — deciding what propagates, on need-to-know, at the source. Hs (the deterministic reduction) and HUF (what passes the distribution breakpoint) are the same architecture seen from two ends. The front end is where they meet: a reduction that is both information-preserving where it matters and governed in what it releases.

5 · Why this is the bet, not the claim

The highest-value seat carries the highest bar. To actually occupy it, the reduction must be shown to preserve what matters — on real data, against independent ground truth, with a domain expert closing the loop — and adoption as a standard is a long game of trust. The honest limits still stand: some higher-order coupling is invisible to any low-D atlas unless overlaps are placed where it lives (the marginal problem), spectra→composition is its own upstream inverse problem, and off-world the calibrating expert-loop is the scarcest ingredient.
