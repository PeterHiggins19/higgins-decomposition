# Archive — talk decks before 13-slide expansion (2026-05-24)

This folder holds the **10-slide compressed talk deck and its companion artifacts**, archived on 2026-05-24 when the conference deck was expanded to 13 slides so each country case-study could carry its navigation chart on a dedicated slide at legible size.

## Why this archive exists

The 10-slide deck (2026-05-20) condensed the previous 22-slide and 12-slide decks into a single sharp conference deck. Each country (Germany / Japan / United Kingdom) lived on one slide that paired the share-and-work figure (~7.6″ wide) with the per-country navigation chart crammed onto the right margin at ~2.6″ wide. The navigation chart at that size was not legible from the back of a CoDaWork conference room.

Peter's directive 2026-05-24: *"slides 6, 7, 8 should have the navigation chart follow dividing from 3 to 6 slides, this was originally done and then it was condensed but now it needs to go back which is fine and the slide talk can stay as is just divide the navigation part to that slide, this will make 13 slides, this is under the recommended 15 slides suggestions, problem is the image is just too small to see and now all slides can be shown large and easier to see."*

The 13-slide deck splits each country's segment into a pair: **share-and-work view** (the existing 4-panel figure at 9″ wide) and **course on the simplex** (the per-country navigation chart at 6.5″ × 5.0″ centered, finally legible from the back). Total deck remains under the 15-slide conference recommendation; the speaking content is unchanged but allocated across the additional slides.
