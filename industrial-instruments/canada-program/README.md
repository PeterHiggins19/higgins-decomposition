## Where Canada fits, in one read

The field's arrow points at **Asia‑Pacific** (~53% manufacturing mass), value concentrated in **US/Taiwan/SK/NL**;
the world is really ~3 blocs (effective dimension ~3.3). **Canada is ~0.2% by mass** — but in the *leading
cluster* by specialty (compound semiconductors, photonics, advanced packaging, AI/quantum), and the only G7
nation without a national semiconductor strategy: a gap that is also an opening.

**The momentum:** Canada can't win on volume, but moving **early on an auditable quality/monitoring standard**
buys influence disproportionate to volume — the model shows an early adopter holding a persistent, compounding
12‑year lead (area ~1.47) over a late one, on the *same* tiny volume share. A head start on the **standard**, not
the **fab**.
