## Verdict

**Yes — the EUV science and concepts can be published. Nothing legally stops them.** They rest entirely on
**public physics** (13.5 nm; photon shot noise / Poisson; the imec two‑sided stochastic cliff; the LPP tin‑droplet
source) plus the **already‑published Hˢ method**. Analysis built on published information and intended to be
published falls under the **fundamental‑research** and **publicly‑available / public‑domain** exclusions to export
control.¹ Nothing in the EUV science is controlled technical data, proprietary process recipe, or non‑public.

The **only** thing held is a **tuned advanced‑node yield recipe** — and that (a) **is not written**, and (b) would
be held by **our own conservative steward choice** (publish the principle, steward the applied, defer upward), **not
a known legal bar.** The business/offer/governance documents are internal **by choice**, because they are strategy,
not science.
