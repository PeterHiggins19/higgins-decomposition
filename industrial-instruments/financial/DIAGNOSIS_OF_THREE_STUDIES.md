*Three views of the financial composition, read by the same instrument, then triangulated. One view is a
point, two a line, three a plane: three studies are the minimum to **locate** a system rather than merely
detect it — the same "three to confirm position" rule the repositories run on. Each reading is
deterministic and reproducible to its hash; the diagnosis is the cross-reading. Descriptive, not advice.

## The triangulation — what three views locate that one cannot

**1 — Directedness agrees across altitude (robust).** At both the detailed sector level (Study 1) and the
macro risk level (Study 3) the market reads **low coherence and low path efficiency** — it is
**rebalancing, not marching**, whichever height you read it from. A property that holds across altitudes
is a property of the system, not of the lens. *The US market over this window is a rebalancing system —
located, not guessed.*

**2 — Complexity is scale-dependent (the churn has an address).** Effective dimensionality falls from
**4.88** (ten sectors) to **1.62** (three super-sectors). The two are not in conflict; they say *the
churn lives at the sector level, while the macro risk posture moves almost one-dimensionally.* An
allocator watching only the risk-on/risk-off dial sees calm — and is correct at that altitude — while the
action is one level down. Reading at two heights locates *where* the complexity sits.

**3 — Regime changes cross-validate.** Study 1's reorganisations fall on trading days
{32, 72, 182, 209, 246}; Study 3's on {32, 80, 100, 194, 204, 209, 224, 246}. **Three coincide exactly —
days 32, 209, 246 — appearing at both altitudes.** A regime change visible at *both* the sector and the
macro level is a **system-wide** reorganisation; one visible at only a single altitude is lens-specific.
The instrument thus separates the market-wide turns (32, 209, 246) from the level-specific ones — a
distinction no single study could make.

**4 — Direction names the tilt.** Study 1: weight to Comm Services / Information Tech / Materials. Study
3: weight to **Sensitive** (which contains those very names), from both Cyclical and Defensive. The two
altitudes agree on the *direction* — a growth/sensitive tilt — and the macro lens compresses the
sector-level arrow into one legible vector. Consistent across scale.

## The located picture

Triangulated, the system sits at a definite place: **a rebalancing (not directed) US market, churning at
the sector level (~5 directions) but drifting gently and almost one-dimensionally toward the
growth/sensitive complex at the macro level, with three genuine market-wide reorganisations (days 32,
209, 246) confirmed by appearing at both altitudes.** That is a *location*, established by three
independent readings agreeing where they should and differing only by scale where they must — which is
exactly how three views pin a point a single view can only suggest.

## The doctrine this demonstrates

Once a study is useful, **three readings locate it.** Not three repetitions — three *different altitudes
or angles* on the same system, whose agreements are robust findings and whose disagreements are
themselves information (here, the scale of the churn). The dissemination strategy is irrelevant to this;
the triangulation is a property of the data processing. A reviewer who follows up finds not one finance
result but three mutually-locating ones, each reproducible to a hash.

## Honest envelope

Every reading is Tier 1 (reproducible); every interpretation is Tier 3 (the expert's). Descriptive only —
no forecast, no probability, **not investment advice**. Public, aggregate data; the staged exchanges hold
no invented numbers. The third study completes the minimum triangulation; more altitudes (a different
grouping, a longer window, a real second exchange) only sharpen the location.
