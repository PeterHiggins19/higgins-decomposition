The temper — science as the declaration of bias, limits, and bounds

Power is worth nothing if it lies. Here is exactly what this will not do.

Its bias

Hˢ reads relative motion — proportions, not magnitudes; how the mix is moving, not whether the market is up or down. It is built for compositions and is honest that it is blind to anything that is not one.

Its limits

It is descriptive, not predictive. It computes no probability, fits no model, forecasts no price, and recommends no trade. It tells you where the system has been and is — never where it will go. This is not investment advice. What the motion means, and any decision, are yours alone. Hˢ is not a financial advisor.

Its bounds

The reading is Tier 1 — verified and reproducible; its meaning is Tier 3 — your judgement. When the data cannot support a read, the instrument holds or warns rather than hand you a confident wrong answer. It runs on public, aggregate data; your private book never leaves your hands. It complements the statistics others publish — it never restates them.

That restraint is the source of the confidence. An instrument that refuses to bluff is one whose every reading you can take to the table.
