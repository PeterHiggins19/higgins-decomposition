The Composition Gauge

A go / no-go gauge for the front door of Hˢ. A few questions, a few numbers — and the logic, step by step, for why you do or don't have a composition. Deterministic; offline; nothing is sent anywhere.

    A worked rejection — baking bread. "How my loaf turns out" is a chaotic process outcome, not data of parts — the rise and crumb depend on temperature, humidity, fermentation, technique. That fails the gauge (NO-GO). But the recipe — flour : water : yeast : salt as fractions of the dough — is a composition (GO), and a run of recipes across batches is a composition in motion. Same kitchen, two answers; the gauge tells which one you brought.

    The higher door — study the studiers. A single market price isn't a composition. But the allocation across sectors is — and so is the composition of forecasts across the analysts who study the market. Don't study the thing; study the composition of those who study it, and read where the system says it's heading. That is how a chaotic field yields a direction: a composition of samples, methods, or observers, in motion.
