## Built, run, tested — and the one result that matters

`kardashev_projection.py` (receipt **`d2dbf604ac3beaca`**, reproduces) plays a Civilization up the ladder,
twice — once governing each domain on its own, once with **Hˢ governing the couplings** (the relations between
domains). The five checks all pass: `CGS = log10(GDoF)`, E1 reproduces HUF's GDoF 48, the climb is monotonic,
and:

> **Hˢ is the bridge.** Governing domains separately, the Civilization tops out at **CGS-4 (CGS 7.56) — it
> governs systems.** Add the Hˢ coupling layer and the *same* Civilization crosses to **CGS-5 (CGS 8.04) ~
> Kardashev V — it governs complexity itself.** The coupling GDoF Hˢ adds is *exactly* what clears the top
> gate.

That is the whole thesis in a game: a coupling is a **relation**, and a relation is only read by the
compositional view. You can be a Kardashev-I civilization with brute domain-by-domain control; you cannot
become a Kardashev-V civilization without reading the ratios between your domains. "Make Civilization better
using Hˢ" turns out to mean: *Hˢ is the rung from governing things to governing how things move together.*
