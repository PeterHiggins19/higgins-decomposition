# Hˢ Induction Map — a graduated path for experts in need

*One door, one gauge, then a graduated induction. You pass a hard go/no-go at the front (do you have a
composition?), then traverse the system at your own pace — orient, fit, run, branch by your discipline
and your purpose, play, and graduate to a verified node. Every stage has its own small gauge: a clear
pass condition before you advance. Deterministic throughout — hash-receipted, honest-tiered, nothing
more or less. Author: Peter Higgins (human authorship for claims); AI-assisted per HUF-STD-001.*

> **How to use this map.** Walk it yourself, or hand it to an AI assistant with the companion
> [`INDUCTION_MAP.json`](INDUCTION_MAP.json) and let it guide you stage by stage, checking each gauge
> before moving on. The map only ever points to real, runnable places in this repository.

---

## ⛩ The front door — the go/no-go gauge (hard pass/fail)

**Gauge:** *Do you have a composition* — parts of a whole tracked in order, within a shared budget?

- **GO →** continue to the induction below. You already have the need; this is your instrument.
- **NO-GO →** with respect, this isn't your best tool. Other methods are better suited and we hope they
  serve you well — no time wasted. (Sorry, compositions only.)
- **HIGHER DOOR →** your raw data isn't a composition, but a composition *of* your samples, methods, or the observers who study your subject might be — form that mix, track it in order, and read its direction.

**Try it interactively → [`COMPOSITION_GAUGE.html`](COMPOSITION_GAUGE.html)** — a few questions and a few numbers, with the reasoning shown at each negation step. The full gauge, with examples and the what-it-does-by-D table: [`IS_Hs_RIGHT_FOR_YOU.md`](IS_Hs_RIGHT_FOR_YOU.md).

## Stage 3 — branch by discipline

Pick the nearest field and open its worked run; each is real public data, reproducible to a hash.

- **Microbiome / longitudinal omics** → [`collaborations/microbiome/`](collaborations/microbiome/) — abundance tables over time; honest null + real signal; lossless to high D.
- **Geoscience / chemostratigraphy** → [`collaborations/geology-wehner/`](collaborations/geology-wehner/) — a mudstone section read in motion; trace-over-bulk; datable boundaries; the field-instrument path.
- **Storage / reliability fleets** → [`collaborations/distributed-systems-backblaze/`](collaborations/distributed-systems-backblaze/) — drive-fleet composition; pre-fault migration.
- **Spaceflight / transcriptomics** → [`collaborations/spaceflight-glds1/`](collaborations/spaceflight-glds1/) — NASA GeneLab; lossless at very high D + an honest global null.
- **Industrial gas / process fluids** → [`industrial-instruments/`](industrial-instruments/) — O₂/CO₂/N₂ life support, produced water, blood/alveolar gas; the MC-4 / ratio-blindness reading.
- **Financial systems** → [`industrial-instruments/financial/`](industrial-instruments/financial/) — the composition that allocates and moves money, read as the system's own vector map; *study the system that studies the market.* Descriptive and deterministic, not statistics, not advice.
- **Energy mixes** → [`experiments/Hs-02_US_Energy`](experiments/Hs-02_US_Energy) · [`experiments/Hs-M02_EMBER_Energy`](experiments/Hs-M02_EMBER_Energy) — transition drift on the simplex.
- **Physics / geochemistry / cosmology** → [`experiments/`](experiments/) (Hs-03 SEMF, Hs-05 Geochemistry, Hs-07 QCD, Hs-16 Planck, …) — validation runs that recover known structure.
- **Experimental mathematics (near dimension four)** → [`papers/frontier/EXACT_DIM4_EXAMPLE_GENERATION.md`](papers/frontier/EXACT_DIM4_EXAMPLE_GENERATION.md) — exact S³=SU(2) / Spin(4) example generation and the high-D chart atlas.

Not listed? If it's a composition, it still runs — Stage 2 takes any compositional table.

## Stage 4 — branch by purpose

What did you come to do? Each purpose maps to a specific component.

- **Read a snapshot** (a static picture only) → the standard-CoDa fallback in [`onramp/PHD_ONRAMP_PROTOCOL.md`](onramp/PHD_ONRAMP_PROTOCOL.md) (ternary / CLR-biplot / variation matrix). Hˢ won't force motion on you.
- **Read the motion** (who steers, the arrow of intent, regime changes) → the full kinematic read, [`Hs-Kinematics/`](Hs-Kinematics/).
- **Monitor** (catch deceptive drift; fail loud, not silent) → the guards and the deceptive-drift detector, [`papers/cnq_tiling_suite_2026/`](papers/cnq_tiling_suite_2026/).
- **Track the budget / control** (is the whole growing or shrinking; a safe closed loop) → [`Hs-Kinematics/MOVING_BUDGET_AND_CONTROL_PRIMITIVE.md`](Hs-Kinematics/MOVING_BUDGET_AND_CONTROL_PRIMITIVE.md).
- **Characterize** (what *kind* of system is this, across domains) → Compositional Character Space, [`library/CCS_FOR_COMPOSITIONAL_READERS.md`](library/CCS_FOR_COMPOSITIONAL_READERS.md).
- **Extend / build** (wire Hˢ into your pipeline) → the tool kit, [`tools/`](tools/), and traceability, [`Hs-Kinematics/TRACEABILITY.md`](Hs-Kinematics/TRACEABILITY.md).
- **Publish / cite** → the paper series, [`papers/`](papers/).

## Stage 5 — the playground

Twenty-five-plus named experiments on real public data, each a self-contained run you can reproduce or
refute. Start anywhere in [`experiments/`](experiments/); the contract every experiment keeps is in
[`experiments/EXPERIMENT_STANDARD.md`](experiments/EXPERIMENT_STANDARD.md). The rule of the playground is
the rule of the whole system: **same data → same result → same hash.** Bring your own composition and it
joins the same playground on the same terms.

## The throughline — determinism, nothing more or less

Every stage is the same instrument seen from a different distance, and every stage obeys one contract:
deterministic, hash-receipted, honest-tiered (Tier 1 verified / Tier 3 open question), and it will hold
or warn rather than hand back a confident wrong answer. The induction adds no claims and removes none —
it only orders the doors. The data is the star; Hˢ is the lens; the receipt is the proof.

*One door, one gauge; then as far as you wish to go, and not one step that isn't reproducible.*
