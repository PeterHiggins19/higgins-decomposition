## The game vs. the world (the fence first, because it's load-bearing)

A war game is a **fixed, forced, zero-sum** contraption: conflict is the *designed* goal, the length is
finite, and you win by elimination. Peter says this plainly — *"the game is fixed, the competition is
forced... it is a game, a fantasy, makes it fun and educational."* That frame is exactly what makes "win by
elimination" fine **there** — and exactly what does **not** carry to a knowledge tool in the real world. Real
adoption is **not** zero-sum, not forced, and not won by removing anyone. So the strategy that transfers is
**only** the part that already assumes you don't destroy: the welcoming, the efficiency, the patience. The
conquest vocabulary stays on the table; the conduct comes with us.

## What legitimately transfers (the horde's actual genius)

The mass-production player didn't win by burning things — he won by **using everything, to the last case, and
making the conquered position better than he found it.** Four moves, each already a HUF/Hˢ principle:

1. **Efficiency to the last case → extract to the last nibble.** The horde's edge was utilizing every
   resource fully, no waste. That is `KNOW_THE_KNOWABLE` exactly: take the finest detail the data yields and
   read it, leave nothing on the table. Same instinct, peaceful target.
2. **No devastation; welcome the former combatant → compose, don't destroy.** Redirect the energies of those
   you meet into a *better place to operate.* In our terms: the **friendly door** and **compose-not-compete**
   — former "competitors" (other schools, other fields) are welcomed and made more capable, not eliminated.
   The CoDa Q-node is this move literally: hand the allies a better instrument, don't conquer their field.
3. **Mass as a shield, not a spear → the trust mass is defensive.** You hold mass because the game *forces*
   conflict on you, not because you seek it — so the mass *protects* the work and deters the fight rather than
   starting it. Our "mass" is the **determinism + receipts + coherence** (pole 2 and 3): an accumulation so
   re-checkable that it doesn't need to attack anything to stand. A shield ends fights; it doesn't pick them.
4. **Steps ahead → the honest edge.** Being ahead of all else, in the game, was foresight. Ours is the
   measured advantage of seeing the ratios others miss (the blindness suite) — earned by depth, not surprise
   attack, and *shown*, not sprung.

## The one transferable mechanism, measured: let entropy do the work

The deepest line — *"dwell time is the problem; keep this up and let entropy do the work for you"* — is the
real strategy, and it is the opposite of conquest. `diffusion_adoption_model.py` models adoption with **every
conquest term removed** and keeps only what's honest (receipt `9984dde5ae92eac8`):

- **There is no force term — it is set to zero and stays zero.** Total force applied: 0.
- Adoption is pure **diffusion down a real gradient**, and the gradient exists **only where the data is
  actually compositional** (need above the door). Result: high-need fields equilibrate upward (0.05 → 0.90)
  with dwell time; off-door fields **do not convert at all** (stay 0.05) — *no gradient, no spread.* The
  friendly door is automatic, not enforced.
- **Saturation is dwell-time-limited:** reaching 0.95 needs **52 steps of contact, more than the 40 given** —
  which is precisely Peter's point. *Dwell time is the lever and the constraint.* Keep the contact up and the
  equilibration completes itself; rush it and it doesn't.
- **"Win by attrition"** is reported honestly as **standing unrealized advantage** — the value a non-adopter
  leaves on the table *where their own data is compositional.* It is their self-chosen cost, never a thing we
  impose. We apply zero force; the gradient is the data's, the choice is theirs.

So entropy here **equilibrates; it does not conquer.** A good idea, made trustworthy even against its author,
spreads down the gradient wherever there is contact — and does nothing where there's no real advantage. That
is diffusion, and it is the whole strategy: be worth adopting, make contact, wait. No spear required.
