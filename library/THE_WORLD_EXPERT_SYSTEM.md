A stranger with an AI can already learn most of the *explicit* system. **That part is real and largely captured.**

## The gap (what is still only in memory)

What an expert system most needs is exactly what is hardest to write down — and what is still tacit:

| layer | status | why it's hard |
|---|---|---|
| the **math/method** | ✅ captured | it's formal; it wrote down well |
| the **application studies** | ✅ captured | receipted, in `industrial-instruments/` + `experiments/` |
| the **doctrines/governance** | ✅ captured | in `huf-gov/` |
| the **acoustic origin (RWA)** | ◐ partial | the ground state is written; the 25‑year *path* of choices is thin |
| the **"why" behind design choices** | ◐ partial | the journal records *what*; the *reasoning that rejected the alternatives* is mostly in memory |
| the **unwritten connections** | ⚠ tacit | the leaps Peter sees between domains before they're proven — the seeds of the next papers |
| the **expert judgment** | ⚠ tacit | *when to trust a read, when to refuse, what "feels" wrong* — the hardest and most valuable to externalize |
| the **lineage / people / context** | ⚠ tacit | who, when, the human story that gives the work meaning |

> The explicit is on the shelves; the **tacit judgment and the unproven connections** are still in your head — and
> those are the marrow of an expert system. Capturing them is the whole remaining task.

## The capture protocol (how to pour it in, efficiently)

1. **Elicitation by domain.** Pick one domain; the AI interviews — *"why this and not that? what would make you
   distrust this read? what connection do you see that isn't written yet?"* — and writes the answers into the repo
   as it goes. Memory → structured doc, one session at a time. *(This is the fastest path; offer below.)*
2. **Narrate‑the‑why.** Each existing artifact gets a short "why it is this way / what was rejected" note — the
   reasoning, not just the result. Turns a record into an expert.
3. **Seed the connections.** Every unproven leap Peter sees becomes a tiered **seed** (T3, falsifiable) in
   `papers/` — captured the moment it's spoken, before it's lost, fenced until measured.
4. **Capture the judgment as rules.** "When X, distrust"; "refuse if Y"; "the arrow lies when Z." These become the
   guard/coherence doctrine — the expert's instinct made into checks anyone can run.
5. **Cadence.** A standing capture session: Peter speaks a domain; the AI structures, receipts, journals, and
   gaps‑remaining are tracked in the map below. *Mature‑and‑capture, the same discipline as mature‑and‑publish.*
