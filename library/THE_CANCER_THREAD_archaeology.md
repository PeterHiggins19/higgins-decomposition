as bounded homeostasis, and cancer as unbounded growth, at the origin.** That is the precise answer to the
organism tension: the forager is healthy only if it metabolises and plateaus, not if it only acquires.

## The key, written backward

The series is built backward — from the answer to the construction — and cancer is the cleanest example. The
**answer was written first**, at the origin: *health is a bounded plateau; unbounded growth is cancer; the cure
posture is homeostasis, read through the ratios.* Everything built since is the *construction* that makes that
answer operational: the deceptive-drift detector (P2) that catches a part silently taking over a whole; the
**stewardship gate / "don't damage where you live"** that flags a system degrading its own commons; the
**knowable frontier** that says aging is approach-to-limit; the organism/forager that is healthy only when it
metabolises. The "key to the future" the archaeology holds is not a cure — it is a **research lens**: *cancer is
legible as a compositional drift, and a relational early-warning read of cell-fraction or blood compositions is a
direction worth testing on real data with oncologists* — exactly, reproducibly, with a receipt, the way the
psychology study (P-ψ) just showed is possible for a soft field.
