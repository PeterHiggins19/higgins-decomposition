## The thesis (and why it's plausible here)

> Understanding the compositional view is not transmitted in a sentence; it **accumulates** — like a
> signature that only locks once you have enough samples. Below the **contact-sample floor** the relational
> picture is below the user's own noise: glazed eyes, *a mountain.* Above it, it locks: they **see**, and
> ratio-blindness falls. (Modeled at `t* ≈` the "seeing" transition; the same knowable-sample floor measured
> in the language read and the stress sheet.) **T3 hypothesis** — "or so goes the theory" — falsifiable by
> measuring real contact-time vs comprehension.

So the communication objective function is **maximize the probability a contact reaches the seeing
threshold** — i.e., keep the user engaged *through* the slow early phase. Contact length is the lever.

## The words and meanings that increase contact length

- **Concrete before abstract, always.** Their data first; the simplex later (or never, if never needed).
- **One thing at a time.** Each contact adds exactly one usable idea, with a payoff. Load is the enemy of
  contact length.
- **Match the realm** (the cheeseburger rule). Never pitch above where the user lives; answer the question
  asked, at the level asked. Pitching the full spectrum buys glaze, not contact.
- **A win every step.** The user must leave each contact with something they can *use or re-check*, so they
  come back — that's how contact accumulates toward the threshold.
- **The friendly door** (composition-is-qualifier). Welcome those with a composition; kindly redirect those
  without. A clean fit keeps the right people in contact and saves the wrong ones' time.
- **No advertisement.** Tell it, show it, offer it for re-computation, then stop. Neediness shortens contact;
  a quiet, re-checkable claim lengthens it.
- **Let them re-compute.** Every claim ships with the public file and the receipt. Verifiability *is* an
  engagement loop — re-running the number is contact.

## Design rules (operational)

1. **Minimise first-contact load; maximise per-step payoff.** The mountain phase is where contacts are lost.
2. **Always leave a next-step hook** — one concrete thing to try, on their data.
3. **Measure contact, not conversion.** The leading indicator is *contact length*, because the theory says
   seeing is downstream of accumulated contact.
4. **The human keeps the gate.** Persuasion never overrides the operator's judgment; coherence is offered,
   never imposed.

## Honest scope

- **T1 (measured):** ratio-blindness is real and named (the blindness suite); the knowable-sample floor is
  measured (language read, stress sheet, max-power).
- **T3 (the theory, "or so goes the theory"):** that comprehension follows a contact-time threshold curve and
  that the right words raise the probability of reaching it. The model is an illustration, not a measurement;
  its falsifier is named (measure real users). The communication *design* built on it is **doctrine** (held
  intent), to be refined by real contact data.
- **Not claimed:** that any specific phrase converts. The claim is structural — *contact length is the lever*
  — not a guarantee about wording.
