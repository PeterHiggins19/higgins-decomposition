## How to keep this current

1. When a new concept or paper enters the work, add a row **and** a row in `package_composition_read.py`
   (id, name, role, tier weights), then re-run it; the receipt changes and the fit re-reads itself.
2. The maturity weights are estimates — revise them when a piece gains a real receipt (T3 → T1) so the
   barycenter read stays honest.
3. This register is the publication-package companion to the operational journal
   (`ai-refresh/HS_TRACKING_LOG.json`): the journal records *what happened*, this records *what we are
   delivering and how it fits.*
