## The goal, in one sentence

> Every other thing in this project — the engine, the guards, the receipts, the instruments, the papers, the
> open release — exists to turn one loop: **science improves the read → a partner improves the data → the
> better data improves the read again.** The goal is to make that loop **ratchet toward the knowable and
> hold every gain,** so the chain of feedback and data improvement becomes a real, cumulative thing rather
> than a pile of one-off results.

## Why this is the goal and not the instruments

The instruments are means. A better compositional read invites a partner — a lab, a company, a government, a
fleet — to collect better data, *because the read makes their data worth more.* That cleaner, larger data
then sharpens the read for everyone. Repeated, this is a **flywheel**: better operations → cleaner data →
better models → better operations. The project's worth is not any single turn; it is whether the turns
**compound** — whether gain feeds gain — and whether the whole thing **stays true** as it grows.

## What makes the chain compound (the thread through the whole project)

Three disciplines, each already built, are exactly what a compounding chain requires:

- **Determinism + receipts → the chain RATCHETS.** Every read is hash-anchored, so a verified gain is never
  silently lost. The loop can only hold or improve; a turn that would regress is caught, not absorbed. This
  is the difference between a chain and a wander.
- **Honest-broker tiers → the chain STAYS TRUE.** Each link is marked for what it is (measured / reasoned /
  vision), so improvements accumulate on solid ground and the chain never quietly inflates past its
  evidence. No link borrows certainty it did not earn.
- **Openness → the chain SERVES ALL.** Publishing the principle invites the widest set of partners to add
  data and science; the applied, tuned versions reward those who help on the way. The loop is global by
  design, with local benefit fenced honestly. (Publish-the-principle / steward-the-applied.)

And the climb has an honest ceiling: the chain converges on **max Q — the knowable limit** — and stops. It
does not manufacture precision below what data and coherence support. Reaching the limit *and knowing it* is
success, not failure.

## Measured — does the loop actually compound? (receipt `5388bc746d33c015`)

Two chains, same noise, 20 cycles, a knowable floor of `0.01`:

| chain | behavior | result |
|---|---|---|
| **anchored / receipted** | each verified gain kept; regressions rejected | **monotone, never regresses; locks at the knowable floor (0.01) in 10 cycles**; data volume compounds ~10,000× |
| **unanchored / drifting** | takes each noisy cycle as-is | **11 regressions**; **4 cycles fake precision *below* the floor** then bounce back; never locks |

The honest reading: the unanchored chain is not merely "higher error" — on a lucky cycle it can look better.
It is **untrustworthy**: it regresses, and it fools itself with false precision below the real limit. Only
the receipted chain **compounds reliably to the truth and stays there.** That is precisely why the project's
determinism discipline is not bureaucracy — it is the mechanism that makes the feedback chain *mean and be
something.*
