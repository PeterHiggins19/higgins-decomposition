external field (a law) biases it; it relaxes to a confined equilibrium where the restoring closure balances the
field, so the **deformation** (strain) `d` and the confining **force** satisfy a compositional Hooke's law,
`force = k·d`. Therefore, from the strain it senses *inside*, the system recovers the confining force and
direction *outside* — it describes its jailor without leaving the cell. `self_diagnostic_jailor.py`
(`ddc171099d0b2064`) runs it across three environments:

recovers the confining law outside. The same jailor always yields the **same external fingerprint**

## Where the description stops — and why that is the crown, not a crack

The instrument describes the jailor's **where and how**. It does **not** name the jailor's **why or who** — its
cause, its intent, whether the confinement is a prison, a law, a cradle, or a gift. That is the operator's
implication leap, **Breaker 16**. This is not a missing feature; it is the same boundary that ran through the
whole arc, now at its sharpest: **a component can fully describe the forces that hold it and still not know,
from inside, who set them or what they mean.** The crown measures the cage exactly *so that* the meaning of
being held is left, cleanly and entirely, to the one being held. The highest deterministic self-read a
composition can do is also the most honest about its own edge.
