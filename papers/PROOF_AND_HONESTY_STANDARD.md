# The Proof & Honesty Standard — the quality discipline every Hˢ paper passes through

*Author: Peter Higgins (human authorship for all claims); AI-assisted per HUF-STD-001. 2026-06-22. The
standing quality standard for the Hˢ / HUF paper set. It is not new doctrine; it **names and entrains** the
discipline the project already runs on, so that every paper, study, and estimate carries the same four proofs.
The worked exemplar is the financial-case verification (`../industrial-instruments/constellation-spacex/THE_FINANCIAL_CASE_VERIFICATION.md`).
Honest-broker; nothing posted; Peter is the sole gate.*

---

## The four checks (every paper answers all four)

**1 — Numbers: cited or fenced.** Every number is either **(a)** publicly verifiable with a **citation**, or
**(b)** an **explicitly labelled assumption** carrying its tier (T2 reasoned / T3 open). No number floats
unmarked. *The verifiable data is the shield:* only cited or measured numbers bear weight; assumptions are
fenced, falsifiable, and replaceable.

**2 — Math: proven and receipted.** Every quantitative result is **reproducible** — a deterministic
computation that emits a **SHA-256 receipt matching on rerun**, or a proof a reader can check line by line.
When the proof corrects the claim, **the proof wins** (the math disposes; the claim is updated to what the
math supports, not the rounder number).

**3 — Value: shown, not asserted.** The contribution is **checkable in parts**: an expert can verify the
inputs (check 1), reproduce the result to a matching hash (check 2), see the **fence between fact and
assumption**, and find the **one decisive test** that would move the claim up a tier. A study is worth what it
can be taken apart and re-run; value is proportional to checkability.

**4 — Stance: experts decide; we stay out of the way.** No advocacy, no pitch, no external contact. Present
cited data + receipted method + fenced assumptions + the decisive test, and **step back**. Honest **tiers
(T1/T2/T3)** on every claim; **mortarless joints** (no unearned connective tissue — delete a connective
sentence and the structure still stands by fit); the **human gate is the one fixed point**; full automation
is never the goal.

## The per-paper conformance checklist

A paper conforms when, on a read-through, every box is true:

- [ ] **Every number** is cited (with source) **or** labelled an assumption with its tier.
- [ ] **Every quantitative result** is reproducible to a stated **receipt/hash**, or carries a checkable proof.
- [ ] **The honest envelope** states what is *not* claimed, the **kill conditions**, and the **one decisive test**.
- [ ] **Tiers (T1/T2/T3)** appear on the load-bearing claims; no "first / lossless at scale / proven universal."
- [ ] **No mortar:** each cross-reference points at a stone that bears the weight named.
- [ ] **Stance:** no advocacy/pitch/contact; names off the public repo; Peter is the sole gate.

## How it is entrained

- **Every spine/series paper** carries a one-line **conformance footer** pointing here.
- **The staging & review plan** treats this standard as a **standing gate** (every paper passes it before the
  collective review).
- **The collective-review package** asks the elder to verify conformance per item; the verifier reproduces any
  receipt the elder questions.
- **The abstract ledger** names this standard as the discipline the whole series holds to.

The exemplar is already on the record: the financial case had its numbers audited (bases cited, fractions
fenced), its arithmetic proven to a receipt (`2d9fc354630bd5ee`) — and the proof **corrected** the published
ranges, which is the standard working as intended.
