## The general law (Tier 1 — measured; the paper's spine)
Under the **geometric-mean temporal-compression operator**, applied to **compositional carriers**, Shannon
entropy is **conserved**: measured variation **< 0.2% (0.18%) across a 341:1 compression ratio**, across
multiple unrelated compositional corpora. This is the one fact the paper exists to establish, with the
**KILL-001 falsifiability** conditions stated up front. The contrast is the hook: most temporal compression
*destroys* information by construction — arithmetic averaging leaks, and even the mean-square-optimal
Karhunen–Loève transform surrenders entropy. EITT says that under this specific operator, on this specific
class of carriers, the structural information rides the compression.

## Why it is omnipresent (the key — one law, many faces)
The paper's distinctive move is to show the same invariance is what powers, separately, things that look
unrelated:
- **Scale-free monitoring.** Real monitoring is multi-scale (seasons and decades; sub-second and multi-year;
  heartbeat and lifetime). Conventional methods must pick a scale and lose the rest; EITT lets you compress
  freely because the information rides along. *(The positive use of the invariance.)*
- **The boundary of the analysable.** Where the invariance **breaks**, you are at the edge of analysable
  compositional structure — the engine's "boundary" verdict is EITT *failing*, not a separate heuristic.
- **The honest-hold.** At that boundary, abstain rather than assert (the localizer that holds instead of
  pointing at a phantom; see `frontier/SPATIAL_HEARING_INVERSE_COMPOSITION_FRONT_END.md`).
- **Vigilance / deceptive drift.** P2's concentration-that-hides detector is one application of watching the
  invariant under compression (`arXiv/P2_deceptive_drift/`).
- **The triangulation boundary leg.** EITT is View C in the three-to-locate protocol — the leg that says
  *whether the location is even analysable* (`papers/TRIANGULATION_PROTOCOL.md`).
- **The cross-scale finding.** Geology read within-regime at full D and **boundary** at the macro
  amalgamation — EITT detecting that coarse-graining carried a located system to the edge
  (`collaborations/geology-wehner/DIAGNOSIS_OF_THREE_VIEWS.md`).

That list **is** the argument: a single, measured invariance appears as monitoring, boundary, abstention,
vigilance, and triangulation. General, and everywhere.

## Where it sits among neighbours (to write)
- **Information theory / compression:** entropy is normally lost under compression (arithmetic mean leaks;
  KLT surrenders entropy). EITT is the named exception for geometric-mean on compositional carriers.
- **Multiresolution analysis (wavelets, scale-space):** they *represent* multiple scales; EITT *preserves the
  information measure across* a scale change — a different, sharper claim.
- **Change-point / novelty / OOD / selective prediction:** these flag *where* something changes or is
  unfamiliar, usually with a trained model; EITT's boundary is **model-free**, from the invariance breaking.
  Position EITT as the deterministic, model-free member of that family.

## Honest scope (contradiction-test reflexes)
- **Not universal entropy preservation.** The invariance is specific to the **geometric-mean operator** on
  **compositional carriers**. Outside that pairing it leaks — and that leakage *is* the boundary signal. Any
  "entropy is preserved under compression" stated without those two qualifiers is **rejected**.
- **The law vs its applications.** The measured invariance (Tier 1) is distinct from how it is used (the
  verdict, the hold). The paper must keep them separate: one is a result, the others are instruments built on
  it. (My own earlier conflation of "EITT" with "the boundary verdict" is exactly the error to avoid.)
- **Kill conditions kept visible.** KILL-001 stays in the paper; the claim is published *with* what would
  falsify it, per the falsifiability discipline.
