**Compositional Character Space: a cross-domain taxonomy of compositional dynamics via the second-order read.**

> We introduce the **second-order read (Hˢ²)**: apply a deterministic compositional-dynamics engine across
> many systems, take each system's symmetry-invariant diagnostic profile as itself a point, and analyze the
> resulting space. Across **107 real longitudinal compositions / 13 domains**, systems sort into four
> recurring **characters** — *Ballistic, Contested, Diffusive, Turbulent* — coherent across unrelated fields;
> **momentum coherence is the principal organizing axis** (53% of variance). We report, as part of the
> result, that an apparent tight dimensional collapse at small sample (n=11) corrects to a milder one at
> scale (n=107) — a falsifiable claim behaving as designed.
