## The method and why it has value

A population's **total** cancer burden is one number; the **composition** — which sites make up that burden — is
a parts-of-a-whole that turns underneath the total. A totals-only view misses the turn; the compositional read
catches it, exactly: `closure → clr → trajectory directedness → motion-helmsman → forward cast → SHA-256
receipt`. This is the **deceptive-drift** idea (paper **P2**) applied to cancer-incidence epidemiology — *cancer
= drift* made measurable: a single site silently taking a larger share of the whole, read in time, with a cast
of where it is heading.

## The demonstration — ILLUSTRATIVE data, clearly labelled

`breast_cancer_composition_demo.py` (receipt `0c44c4a150cad7f0`) runs the method on a female cancer-site
composition {Breast, Cervix, Ovary, Oral cavity, Other}. **The data is illustrative / synthetic, parameterized
only to the documented qualitative direction** reported in the literature — in India, breast cancer has risen to
become the leading female cancer while the cervical-cancer share has declined. **No real registry percentage is
asserted.** On that illustrative series the instrument reads the transition exactly: trajectory directedness
**0.626**; the breast share **rising** (0.18 → 0.32, 10-year cast 0.39); the cervical share **falling** (0.26 →
0.10, cast 0.06); the fastest-moving part (motion-helmsman) the cervical decline, with breast its rising
counterpart. The point is the *method*: it sees the composition turn while a totals view would not.
