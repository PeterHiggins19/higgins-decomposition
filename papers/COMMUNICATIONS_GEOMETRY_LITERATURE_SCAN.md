## The question

Hˢ claims: a composition (parts of a conserved whole) *is* a message; the discriminative signal lives in the
inter-part ratios; and the more parts / higher the dimension, the more communication symbology it carries. Is
this already known? Where does each piece stand, and where does it only crawl?

## What is ADJACENT but not joined

- Ecology uses **scalar** Shannon diversity as "the information of the community"; comms uses **vector**
  N-dimensional constellations as "the information of a signal." **No one has joined them** by reading the
  *community's composition* as an *N-dimensional constellation whose capacity grows with the number of parts.*
- The compositional-data literature has the log-ratio geometry but, per the web check, **does not link it to
  channel capacity** — that connection is absent.
