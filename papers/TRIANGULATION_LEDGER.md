## How to fill a third leg (the recipe, ~one run each)

1. **View B (Structure)** — amalgamate the domain's parts into 3–5 super-parts at the meaningful macro
   grouping (finance: cyclical/sensitive/defensive; geology: detrital/carbonate/organic; energy:
   fossil/renewable/nuclear; microbiome: phylum). Read at the low-D exact end. One run.
2. **View C (EITT/vigilance)** — the engine already returns the EITT boundary verdict on every run; record
   it, and add the deceptive-drift check. No new code.
3. **Diagnosis** — cross-read A/B/C per the protocol: agreements (system properties), disagreements (scale
   info), cross-validated regimes (system-wide vs lens-specific), EITT verdict (on solid ground or near
   the edge).

Most domains are one View-B run and a short diagnosis away from being located. That work is **the
expert's to extend and control** — the ledger only makes the gap visible.

## The scale test

This ledger is itself a composition: domains as parts, "located-ness" as the budget each holds. Read that
way, the repository is the experiment — *can the three-to-locate method be carried across every domain a
compositional instrument can reach?* Finance says yes for one domain, fully; geology says the second leg
generalises; the rest are staged, honestly, awaiting their third view. The instrument that triangulates a
market triangulates a mudstone, a microbiome, a fleet — and the ledger tracks the proof accumulating.
