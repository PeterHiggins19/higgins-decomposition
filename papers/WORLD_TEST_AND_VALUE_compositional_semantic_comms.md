## The concept under test

> Communicate using the data **organized to tell us about itself** — the control (what to acquire, how to
> transmit, how to decode) is **intrinsic to the data's compositional geometry**, not carried as separate
> symbology. For communications this removes the wasted control channel; for any compositional system, the
> same engine **reads its messages in its own composition.** All deterministic, with total coherence of purpose
> and result.

## Where this lands in the world (established / active — Hˢ inherits, does not claim)

| the idea | where it stands | source |
|---|---|---|
| **Three levels of communication: technical (bits) · semantic (meaning) · effectiveness (goal/action).** The Duplex operates at the **semantic + effectiveness** levels — the instruction is *understood* and *acted on*. | **Established** (Shannon–Weaver 1949), **actively revived** for 6G. | Weaver levels; 6G semantic-comms surveys |

So the *goal* Peter described is not a fringe idea — it is the **headline direction of 6G communications
research** (semantic/goal-oriented) and the decades-old **in-band-signaling** tradition. The Duplex is a
concrete instance of goal-oriented semantic communication.

## What is DIFFERENT — and where the value is (the corner no one occupies)

The semantic / goal-oriented field is **overwhelmingly deep-learning based**: neural semantic encoders/decoders
that are **learned, lossy, probabilistic, opaque, and unverifiable** (no exact reconstruction, no audit, model
drift). That is the mainstream *and its weakness* for high-stakes links. Hˢ occupies the corner the mainstream
does not:

- **Deterministic & exact**, not learned/lossy — same input → same output → same receipt.
- **Control intrinsic to the geometry**, not metadata (in-band signaling) and not a trained model: the
  composition tells you about itself *by construction* (closure + log-ratio), so acquisition/transmission/decode
  are governed by the data's own structure.
- **Self-verifying** — every hop hash-stamped; the origin re-derives the far-end computation without trusting it.
- **Self-protecting** — closure rejects common-mode gain exactly (the channel can't corrupt the level).
- **Interpretable** — the read is an arrow/character/effective-dimension, not a black-box embedding.

> **The value, stated honestly:** what's been built is a **deterministic, exact, hash-verified instance of
> goal-oriented semantic communication on compositional data** — in the live 6G "beyond-Shannon" paradigm, but
> in the corner that paradigm leaves open: *control intrinsic to the data's relational geometry, and the whole
> exchange re-runnable and auditable.* Its value is **highest exactly where the dominant neural semantic-comms
> approaches are weakest** — regulated, safety-critical, space, fleet-bus, or any link that must be **verified
> rather than trusted**. And as a bonus the same engine, pointed at any compositional system, reads that
> system's message in its own composition — communications and analysis are one operation.

## The honest caveats (so the value survives a reviewer)

- **Semantic/goal-oriented communication is a large, well-funded field.** Hˢ does **not** own it and is **not
  "first."** "Transmit meaning not bits" has hundreds of papers.
- **The Duplex is a simulated protocol**, not a deployed system, and **not yet benchmarked** against neural
  semantic codecs on standard tasks. The decisive comparison (rate/fidelity/energy vs a learned semantic codec
  on a real task, with the determinism+audit advantage quantified) is the named next test.
- **No information-theoretic limit is beaten.** The advantage is determinism, integrity, interpretability, and
  the compositional substrate — not capacity beyond Shannon.
