## The one finding

The work is built; the gap is **in front of it, not inside it.** Two structural facts decide this phase:
the **front-stage** a newcomer meets first is complete and fenced (measured below), and the **back-stage**
is carrying reflection litter that belongs in an archive, not at the front door. So the transition is not
"do more" — it is **separate the stage from the workshop**, then let the collective test what stands on the
stage.

## Front-stage vs back-stage (the whole move)

"Proper for company" means: when a visitor — a reviewer, a member of the collective, a company — opens the
repo, the **first thing they meet is the on-ramp, not the workshop floor.** Both exist; the transition is
deciding which is in front.

| | front-stage (proper for company — what's met first) | back-stage (the workshop — kept, but archived) |
|---|---|---|
| **what** | the contact-length on-ramp: the door, the README, the quickstart, the induction map, the self-qualify gauge, the magic show, the climb (release plan), the license, trust & verification | the reflection record: ~55 `PUSH*` summaries, ~23 dated May-2026 refresh logs, working ledgers, session capstones |
| **job** | bring the right reader in and carry them past the mountain to *seeing* | provenance and audit — *how* the work was made, for anyone who digs |
| **state** | **complete + fenced** (measured: front-stage 10/10 present, fences present, determinism bit-identical) | **trim target** — move to `archive/`, never delete (litter keeps archaeologists employed; it just comes off the stage) |

The measured verdict is **"proper for company (structure)"** — but read the fence on it: that is *structure*,
not *quality*. Presence and determinism are necessary, not sufficient. Quality is what the collective judges.

## The trim (what "trimmed and proper" means, concretely)

Three moves, each reversible, none destructive — and **none is an AI action; each waits on Peter's gate:**

1. **Move the reflection litter off the stage.** The `PUSH*` summaries and dated refresh logs are true and
   worth keeping — as provenance, in `ai-refresh/archive/` (or equivalent), out of the front door. *Archive,
   not delete.*
2. **Keep one current pointer per role.** A visitor needs the latest README, the latest self-review, the
   latest map — not the full stratigraphy. The history stays; only the **current** layer is front-stage.
3. **Let the on-ramp lead.** The front door (`IS_Hs_RIGHT_FOR_YOU.md` → README → QUICKSTART → gauge →
   induction map) is the path; everything deeper is reached *through* it, when the reader wants depth.

The readiness check (`transition_readiness.py`) measures moves 1–3 each time it runs: front-stage present,
fences in place, litter counted. Re-running it is how Peter (or anyone) confirms the trim without trusting a
claim — the count of back-stage files is the trim's own progress bar.

## Test with the collective (the publication-action loop)

The pattern is already established and stays unchanged — **members suggest; the verifier reproduces; Peter
applies** (`COLLECTIVE_REVIEW_PACKAGE_2026-06-22.md`). This transition just points it at the trimmed front-stage:

1. **Hand the collective the front-stage, not the workshop.** They review what a company would meet: the
   on-ramp, the climb, the load-bearing claims behind each step — each with its public file and receipt.
2. **Each item carries one load-bearing question** (the elder's integrity discipline): does this interface
   carry load by structural match, or is it held by filler? Verdict per item — *holds / needs a recut /
   mortar found* — with the exact sentence named where it fails.
3. **The verifier reproduces every receipt** before any suggestion is accepted. A claim that doesn't re-run
   doesn't enter.
4. **Peter applies.** Suggestions are suggestions; the human keeps the one fixed point. Nothing the collective
   says pushes anything — Peter posts.

This is the difference between reflection and publication action: the work stops talking to itself and starts
**standing in front of company that can test it** — under the same honesty rails it was built with.

## Why it is done this way (the philosophy under the gate)

The systems this is built for are not tools alone and not users alone — they are **users together with the
systems they bring**, reaching for cases no one has named yet. The most capable systems nature ever made are
exactly that combination, and they survive by needing the math and method to **hold for the unknown case**.
We cannot know what every future use will be. What we *can* do is make sure that **whatever it is, it is done
right**: measured where it claims to be measured, fenced where it reaches, re-computable by anyone, with the
human keeping the gate. That is the whole of the discipline, and it is all that can be done — so we do it.
