Here is the organising idea we offer, exploratory and stated as such. In a coupled-resonator system, the
coherence of the whole is set by its nodes, each with its own **quality factor Q**; the lowest-Q node is the
**helmsman of coherence** (Thiele–Small; the Hˢ `Q` keystone). A research community is also a system of
coupled contributors — and, being a set of parts contributing to one body of knowledge, it is itself a
**composition**, readable by the very tool under discussion (the recursion is not rhetorical; it is the

In that picture, **Hˢ asks to be a Q node** — not a centre, not a replacement, but one contributor that

collective Q. A node lifts a coupled system only by being coherent itself and by coupling honestly; that is
