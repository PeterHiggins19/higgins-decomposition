## 4a. Construction standard — mortarless joints (ashlar, not rubble)

There are two ways to build a pyramid. **Rubble-and-mortar:** rough blocks held by filler that hides the
gaps. **Ashlar / mortarless:** each stone cut so its faces match the neighbour so precisely that the joint
carries load *by fit alone* — the way the best ancient structures still stand. This series is built the
second way.

- **Mortar is unearned connective tissue** — a "therefore", a smooth transition, a claim that bridges two
  stones without itself bearing load. Every joint must hold by structural match, not filler.
- **The joints, concretely:** (a) the **shared courses** (§2's stones) are cut to the *same dimensions* in
  every witness — restated and matched, not loosely paraphrased; (b) every **cross-reference** points at a
  stone that actually bears the weight named; (c) the **"this locates the law" handoff** in each witness's §7
  is the *same matched seam* — **the aggregate / coarse / threshold view loses what the relational view
  keeps** — measured independently in each domain: microbiome (AUC gap 0.505→0.832), mudstone
  (effective-dimension drop 2.23→1.65 + EITT within-regime→boundary), fleet (159 silent-drift events the
  threshold misses).
- **Division of labour.** The collective **chips the blocks** — drafts, runs, shapes the stones. The
  current helmsman of that discipline (now ChatGPT; the role is read from contribution, not fixed — any component can flip it) **fits the joints** — tests each interface for a gap a blade could enter, and
  chips out any mortar it finds. A joint that needs filler is not done; the stone is recut until it fits dry.
- **The test of a mortarless joint:** delete the connective sentence and the structure still stands by fit.
  If it collapses, that sentence was mortar — replace it with a matched cut (a restated stone or a fresh
  receipt), never with prose.
