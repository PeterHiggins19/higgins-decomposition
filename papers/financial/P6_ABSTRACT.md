**Compositional Navigation in Finance: a deterministic kinematic reading of allocation systems on the
Aitchison simplex.**

> A portfolio, a desk, an index, a flow ledger — any allocation is a *composition*: parts of a whole. We read
> an observed allocation trajectory as deterministic kinematics (arrow, helmsman, character, effective
> dimensionality, dated regimes) on the Aitchison simplex — a descriptive, hash-receipted instrument, not a
> model, optimiser, or forecast. Demonstrated on a real public S&P-500 sector run; the honest null is
> reported (suggestive, not anomalous).
