## The mathematical lineage (the obstruction is the construction)

The pattern is one of the deepest in mathematics. The same object that **measures a failure** **classifies the
construction** — and in each field it has a name.

| field | the diagnostic (the flaw) | the same object as construction (the proof) |
|---|---|---|
| **Obstruction theory** (Eilenberg, Steenrod, mid-20th c.) | the **obstruction cocycle** measures the failure to extend a map over the next skeleton | its **cohomology class is zero iff the extension exists** — the obstruction *is* the existence certificate |
| **Galois theory** (Galois, 1832) | the **non-solvability of the Galois group** obstructs solution by radicals | the *same* group structure **classifies and constructs** the solvable cases; embedding obstructions are computed as cohomology classes |
| **Noether's theorem** (Noether, 1918) | — | every **symmetry (invariance)** *is* a **conservation law**; the invariance that defines the structure constructs the conserved quantity. Invariance ⟺ a constructive invariant |
| **Klein's Erlangen program** (Klein, 1872) | what is *not* invariant under the group is not part of the geometry | **geometry is the study of the invariants** of a transformation group — the same group analysis diagnoses and constructs |
| **Gödel incompleteness** (Gödel, 1931) | the **diagonal/self-reference** finds a sentence the system cannot prove | the *same* diagonal **constructs** that sentence — the falsifier is built, not merely shown to exist |
| **Control / spectral** (Nyquist, 1932; resonance) | a **pole / eigenvalue / resonance** is where the system *fails* (instability) | the *same* transfer-function analysis **designs the stabiliser**; the pole is the design datum — the closest engineering kin to this project's loudspeaker/Q lineage |

Across all of them the diagnostic and the constructive are **one analysis of one structure** — exactly the
duality. Obstruction theory is its cleanest formal statement: *the obstruction class is the existence proof.*

## The honest contrast — and the project's contribution

There is also a classical form to contrast against: **reductio ad absurdum** (Euclid onward) — finding the
contradiction *is* the proof, but **non-constructively**: it shows *that* something must be so without
*building* it. The Diagnostic-Constructive Duality as it operates here is the **constructive** cousin: it does
not merely show a flaw exists, it **locates** it (which coordinate, which route, which band), and the location
*is* the construction.

What makes that possible — and what is the project's actual contribution, claimed honestly — is **determinism
on real data**: because every read is exact and receipted, the obstruction is not an abstract cohomology class
but a **located, hash-stamped fact** on a real composition. The idea is ancient (obstruction theory, Noether,
Galois); the contribution is the **deterministic, located, receipted instance** of it — "the obstruction is
the construction," made operational and exact on real compositional data, where the located flaw is
immediately the constructive fix (clr → ilr; the recovering channel; the outlier route).
