## Visual (described)

- Author line: Peter Higgins — Markham, Ontario, Canada
- Centre: a stylised simplex with the nine carrier vertices labelled — Coal, Gas, Oil, Nuclear, Hydro, Wind, Solar, Biomass, Other
- One sample composition shown as an interior point with the carrier shares listed as a vector summing to 1

## Spoken text

> Energy generation is a composition. A country's electricity mix in a given year is a vector of carrier shares that sum to one. Coal, gas, oil, nuclear, hydro, wind, solar, biomass, other — nine carriers, on the eight-simplex.

> Standard compositional data analysis gives us the right machinery: closure to put each year on the simplex, perturbation to move from one year to the next as a relative change rather than a difference, Aitchison distance to measure how big that change is, and a concentration measure related to effective diversity to read how spread out the mix is.
