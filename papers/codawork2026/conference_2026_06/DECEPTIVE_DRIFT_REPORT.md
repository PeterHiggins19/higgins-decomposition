## Reading

- A country fires the protocol if **both** conditions hold at the pre-shock year (2021): K_eff declining steeply enough AND structural velocity (TV) below series median (the "deceptive" signature — concentration shifting while overall step-to-step change stays quiet).
- The permutation p-value is the empirical frequency that the pre-shock detection still fires under random reordering of the YoY/TV series. Low p ⇒ the observed pattern is improbable under random reordering, addressing the packet's explicit absence of a formal null.
- This is the annual-grain analogue of the packet's monthly 6-month sliding window. A direct monthly reproduction (which would yield the packet's reported p = 0.0016 number) requires the monthly EMBER pipeline and is queued as a follow-on module.
