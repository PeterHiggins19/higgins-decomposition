## The strong validations (where a talk is our thesis, in their words)

- **Our blindness / ratio-blindness thesis ↔ "The cost of ignoring compositionality."** *Narayana JK,
  Chotirmall SH — "The cost of ignoring compositionality: CoDA unmasks treatment effects in COPD clinical
  trials" (p. 32).* This is the community stating, on real COPD clinical-trial data, exactly what our blindness
  suite and the world-composition map argue: read the totals and you miss what lives in the ratios. A direct,
  independent corroboration from the field.
- **Our MRI / medical study (G-277) ↔ compositional neuroimaging, in the keynotes.** A keynote on **early brain
  changes in Alzheimer's Disease** uses *neuroimaging measures* read compositionally for gene discovery
  (keywords: neuroimaging measures, Alzheimer's disease, gene discovery, p. 4-5) — and **Health** features
  cancer epidemiology (*Kanjiradan Veetil A, Dilip TR, "Integrating compositional data analysis in cancer
  epidemiology," p. 28*), survival from **mutational signatures** (*Galván-Femenía I, Palarea-Albaladejo J,
  p. 24*), renal-disease biomarkers (compositional approach), nutrition (*Morimoto J, "Why nutrition data are
  compositional," p. 35*), and an invited talk on **"the … future of health science."** The community is
  already reading derived medical/brain measures as compositions — exactly the honest line our MRI study drew.
- **Our energy / Canada governance (G-273/G-274) ↔ Higgins' own energy-mix talk + the essential-zeros note.**
  The program explicitly flags that *country-level energy-mix composition is where essential zeros naturally
  appear* — the very issue our re-run harness surfaced (early-solar structural zeros breaking exact invariance).
