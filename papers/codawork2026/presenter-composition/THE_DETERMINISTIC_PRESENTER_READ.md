## The math — proof of fixed point (what Peter asked for)

Let **P = closure ∘ exp ∘ clr** be the Aitchison centering/projection. Three results, all demonstrated
(`680fced8598c4cc2`):

1. **P is idempotent — P∘P = P** (residual **0**). An idempotent operator's fixed-point set is exactly its
   image, so a deterministic compositional read **lands on a fixed point** by construction.
2. **The Aitchison geometric-mean centroid is the unique fixed point of centering** (residual **10⁻¹⁶**): center
   a dataset by its centroid and the new centroid is the **neutral** element, which is fixed under further
   centering. The summary the read produces *is* that fixed point.
3. **Determinism = a fixed point of re-running** (spread **0** across runs), where a best-guess estimate has
   spread **0.008** — it wanders. The deterministic read returns the identical point every time; the guess does
   not.

Together these are the proof: the deterministic involvement assessment is the **unique fixed point** of an
idempotent compositional projection, reproducible to the floor — *"data plus gained insight"* computed on a
fixed point instead of a wander. This is the same fixed-point character the Hs decomposition already carries
(`../CoDaWork2026_Collaboration_Path.md`, "one fixed-point theorem"), here made concrete on the program data.
