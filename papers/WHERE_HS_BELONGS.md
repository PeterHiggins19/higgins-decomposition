## 2. Who needs Hˢ — the two-part qualifier (the intersection is the home)

A field needs Hˢ when **both** are true:

1. **The data is a composition** — parts of a conserved whole, where the information is in the *ratios*
   (the qualifier gate; if it isn't compositional, Hˢ says so and steps aside).
2. **The output must be reproducible / auditable / exact** — a regulator, insurer, court, counterparty, or
   reviewer must be able to **re-run it and get the same answer**, or a confident-wrong answer is unacceptable.

Most tools satisfy one or the other. The **intersection** — compositional *and* audit-critical — is thinly
served, and it is exactly where determinism stops being a nicety and becomes a requirement. **That is the
home.**

## 4. The highest-value output we can offer

Not "an answer" — a **trustable** answer: the exact relational read **plus** the determinism receipt **plus**
the withholding guard that says *"here the evidence runs out."* For audit-critical compositional fields, the
scarce thing is not a number; it is a number a third party can **re-run to the bit and know when not to
believe.** That triad — exact · receipted · honestly bounded — is the highest-value output, and it is the one
less-capable, noisier tools structurally cannot give.

## 5. How to make the noise — honestly, so it carries

The way a careful newcomer cuts through the din is **not** louder claims; it is **receipts a skeptic can
reproduce**:

1. **Publish the three witnesses** — one law, located across living/geological/engineered systems, each
   receipted. Repetition across independent domains *is* the signal.
2. **Ship one decisive prototype that isn't ours yet** — the storm-backtest (a measured *hours-of-warning*),
   or the dual-quaternion 6-DOF demo. A single new receipt out-argues any positioning statement.
3. **Hand over the determinism contract** — HS-EPS-1 and the four-form open implementation, so anyone can
   reproduce the headline hash and stop trusting and start checking.
4. **Lead every claim with its tier** — the honest tiers are the credibility, not a hedge: a field that has
   been burned by confident-wrong tooling trusts the one that says where it stops.

The distinctive sound is **a clean, reproducible result that holds when a hostile reviewer re-runs it.** That
is the noise worth making, and it is the only kind that does not fade.
