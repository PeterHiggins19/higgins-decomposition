# Contradiction-test protocol — the system tests itself by what cannot both be true

*A standing practice, sibling to the triangulation protocol and EITT. Where triangulation finds *where* to
look and EITT marks the boundary of the analysable, the contradiction test decides *which claim dies* when
two cannot both hold. It is the cheapest, most decisive test available — internal, needing no new data,
only consistency. This is what makes the honest-broker stance a **practice** and not just a posture:
falsification by construction. Author: Peter Higgins (human authorship for claims); AI-assisted per
HUF-STD-001. Honest-broker.*

---

## The principle

In a deterministic system with stated claims, **two mutually exclusive claims cannot both be true.** If one
side is already established at Tier 1 (measured, reproduced), the other is thereby **refuted — at zero
external cost.** A claim that can only be true if an established result is false carries its own refutation;
you do not need to go and verify it. Contradiction is proof of inconsistency: at least one statement is
false, and the measured one wins.

This is Popperian falsification made operational and *internal*. Most verification reaches outward (run it
again, on another machine, against another dataset). The contradiction test reaches *inward*: does this new
claim survive co-existence with what we already hold? Many over-reaching claims fail here, instantly,
before any external effort is spent.

## The move (the practice)

For every new claim — especially imported frontier or collective material — do this **before** accepting or
verifying it:

1. State the claim as a sharp proposition.
2. Find whether it is **mutually exclusive** with something the system already holds at Tier 1.
3. If it is, the claim is **rejected by the contradiction itself**; cite the pair and stop. No external
   verification is owed to a self-contradictory claim.
4. If no contradiction is found, it proceeds to ordinary tiered verification (it is not thereby *true* —
   only *not yet refuted*).

The discipline is symmetric and honest: the same test that kills an over-reach would kill one of our own
claims if it conflicted with a measured fact. The register below is therefore a list of **live tripwires**,
not rhetoric.

## Canonical worked instance — exactness ⟺ flatness

The principal-bundle thread (Grok, 2026-06-18) reached for non-trivial curvature, holonomy, and instanton
numbers over the chart atlas. The contradiction test rejected it without any new computation:

> The atlas reconstructs the clr state **exactly** precisely because parallel transport is
> **path-independent**. Path-independent transport is, by definition, a **flat** connection — **zero
> curvature**. A non-zero curvature would make transport path-dependent, i.e. reconstruction would **fail**.
> Therefore *"the curvature generates instanton numbers"* and *"reconstruction is exact"* **cannot both be
> true.** The exactness *is* the flatness. The established side (exact reconstruction, Tier 1, 5-way
> conformance) stands; the topological super-structure is refused — by contradiction, not by taste.

## How it relates to the other protocols

- **Triangulation** finds *disagreements across altitudes* — they flag **where** to look. A contradiction
  test then decides **which** of two claims dies. Disagreement is information; contradiction is a verdict.
- **EITT** marks where structure stops being analysable; a contradiction test operates on **claims**, not
  data — it can run even where EITT says "boundary."
- **Claim tiers** supply the ammunition: only a **Tier-1** (measured, reproduced) statement is strong enough
  to refute its opposite. Two Tier-3 claims in tension are merely open questions, not a contradiction test.

## Operating rule (enforced)

1. No frontier or collective claim is accepted into a paper or a doctrine until it has been run against this
   register. A claim that contradicts an established side is **rejected and recorded**, with the pair named.
2. The register is **maintained**: each newly found mutually-exclusive pair is added, with its established
   side and what it rejects. The register growing is the science accumulating.
3. The test is applied to **our own** claims with the same edge. If one of ours ever contradicts a measured
   fact, ours is the one that falls.

*Contradiction is not an opinion about a claim; it is a proof that two claims cannot share a world. The
measured one keeps the world. That is the practice, not just the theory.*

## Scope note on C-1 — "curvature" is bundle-specific

C-1 rejects non-zero curvature **for the compositional reconstruction atlas**, where exact (path-independent)
transport forces a flat connection. It does **not** reject physical gauge curvature in other bundles: e.g.
electromagnetism is a $U(1)$ gauge theory whose curvature $F=dA$ **is** the field and is genuinely non-zero.
Same word, different bundle. When applying C-1, name the bundle: the reconstruction atlas (flat, by
exactness) vs. a physical gauge field (curvature = the physics). See
`frontier/LIGHT_AS_COMPOSITION_RIEMANN_SILBERSTEIN.md`.
