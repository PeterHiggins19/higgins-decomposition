## Why nine, and what it proves

Three is enough to *locate* the law (a microbiome, a mudstone, and a fleet that cannot have agreed in advance).
**Nine** makes each location a *trust*: a main result, a replication or orthogonal class, and a dimensional or
null validation — so no single witness rests on one run. Across the chain the recurring, measured fact is the
thesis: **the discriminative signal is in the relational structure and grows with the number of parts**, while
the scalar/aggregate read is blind. A new method must support itself; these nine studies are that support,
each receipted, each honest about the *type* of support it offers (replicate · dimensional · independent ·
orthogonal · null).
