## The observability law (the core, measured)

A composition under closure is a **sensor of its own confinement.** An external environment imposes a
confining field; the composition deforms; from the deformation it recovers the environment. The measured law
of *how much it can tell*:

> **Recoverable knowledge of the outside ≈ (mesh − 1) × precision, where precision grows as
> contact × √dwell ⁄ noise.**

Two halves, both measured (`1d7d3c12657c4f08`):

- **Precision comes from dwell × contact.** Recovering a known environment field, the relative error falls as
  more looks accumulate and coupling strengthens — from cos 0.14 (one weak look) to **cos 1.000** at high
  dwell and contact (error 6.9 → 0.02). The reason is *compositional coherence*: closure + the locked-
  discriminant invariance make each deformation a **faithful, reproducible** record of the environment, so
  repeated looks **accumulate instead of cancelling**, and the read sharpens as **κ·√T**.
- **Resolution comes from the size of the mesh.** Sensing the *same* environment through a coarse mesh
  (parts aggregated) vs a fine one: the coarse mesh recovers less — cos **0.26 (2 parts) → 0.39 → 0.65 → 0.98
  (10 parts)**. A **D-part mesh resolves at most D−1 independent directions** of the outside. Mesh size *is*
  how many independent things about the environment you can distinguish.

So determinism buys a **full-dimensional picture** of the outside, and the picture's *sharpness* is bought with
dwell and contact, its *dimension* with mesh. That is the core math under the self-diagnostic, the jailor, and
the governance read alike.
