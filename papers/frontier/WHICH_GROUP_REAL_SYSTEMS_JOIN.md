words: *"compositional dynamics on the simplex is exactly the case where all three quaternion invariances

trajectory turns out to satisfy.

## Closing the loop — why *this* group, via the ladder break

The companion construction `THE_LADDER_AND_THE_BREAK.md` shows the division-algebra ladder ℝ→ℂ→ℍ→𝕆→𝕊

- D=4 systems join a genuine **group, SU(2) = S³** — because ℍ is the last associative division algebra;

- higher D leaves the sphere-group world entirely and needs **Clifford algebras**.

The break in the ladder and the membership in SU(2) are one theorem seen from two sides: *real systems
belong to SU(2) because that is the largest sphere the algebra still lets be a group.* The dig and the
construction meet.
