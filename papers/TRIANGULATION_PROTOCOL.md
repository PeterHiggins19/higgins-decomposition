# The triangulation protocol — three altitudes locate a system; EITT guards the edge

*A method refinement applied across the whole repository: once a study is useful, **three readings
locate it**. Not three repetitions — three *different altitudes or angles* on the same real composition,
whose agreements are system properties, whose disagreements are scale information, and whose boundary
(EITT) tells you whether the location is even on solid ground. One view is a point, two a line, three a
plane — three is the minimum to *locate* rather than merely *detect*. This is the same "three to confirm
position" rule the repositories run on, now applied to the studies themselves. The composition extends as
the expert desires and controls; the whole repository is the test of this at scale. Author: Peter Higgins
(human authorship for claims); AI-assisted per HUF-STD-001. Honest-broker.*

---

## The three views (the standard triad for every domain study)

**View A — Navigation (full detail).** Read the composition at its native dimension D: helmsman, arrow
of intent, character (diffusive↔directed), effective dimensionality, dated regime changes. *Where the
system is, in detail.*

**View B — Structure (macro / sub-composition).** Amalgamate the parts into a few super-parts (or read a
chosen sub-composition) at the low-D, exact end. *The coarse balance — what the detail rolls up to.*

**View C — Boundary & vigilance (EITT).** Run the **EITT** entropy-invariant boundary test: is the
reading *within-regime* (coherent structure the deterministic read can resolve) or *near the edge* (the
instrument running out of resolution)? Add the deceptive-drift / monitoring check. *Is the location on
solid ground — and is anything quietly drifting?*

Then the **Diagnosis** cross-reads A, B, C:
- **Agreements across altitude** (e.g. directedness low at both D and low-D) ⇒ a property of the
  *system*, not the lens — a robust, located finding.
- **Disagreements** (e.g. effective dimensionality falling from detail to macro) ⇒ *scale information* —
  they tell you *where* the complexity lives.
- **Cross-validated regimes** — reorganisations that appear at more than one altitude are *system-wide*;
  single-altitude ones are lens-specific. (Finance: days 32, 209, 246 appear at both altitudes.)
- **EITT verdict** — if any view is near the boundary, the location is flagged as resting on thin ice
  (Tier 3, honestly); if all hold within-regime, the triangulation stands on coherent structure.

## Why EITT is the third leg, not an afterthought

Triangulation is only meaningful where the system is analysable. EITT is the guard that says so: it
decimates the composition by geometric mean and asks whether the information survives. **Within-regime**
⇒ the three views are locating a real structure. **Near-boundary** ⇒ the views may be triangulating
noise, and the diagnosis must say so. So EITT is not decoration — it is the honesty boundary that keeps
"three to locate" from becoming "three ways to fool yourself." (Tier 3 always — a clue, never a claim.)

## Claim tiers

Each view's numbers are **Tier 1** (verified, reproducible to a hash). The triangulated *location* is
**Tier 2** (a sound cross-reading). EITT and any interpretation are **Tier 3** (the expert's). No view
asserts a forecast; all are descriptive.

## Extensibility — the expert controls the composition

The triad is the *minimum*, not the maximum. The expert who owns the domain adds altitudes at will: a
different amalgamation, a longer window, a second real instance, a sub-composition of interest. Each new
altitude that agrees sharpens the location; each that disagrees adds scale information. The composition of
studies is itself a composition the expert closes and reads — which is the recursion the whole system
runs on (`library/THE_ARCHITECTURE_AS_COMPOSITION.md`). **The repository is the test of this at scale:**
every domain folder is a candidate triad, and the ledger (`TRIANGULATION_LEDGER.md`) tracks which are
located and which await their third view.
