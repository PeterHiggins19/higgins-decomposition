## Where it starts: a loudspeaker

Almost everything here began with a box radiating sound into a room. A loudspeaker, at low frequencies, sends energy equally in every direction; as the pitch rises, that balance breaks and the energy concentrates forward. The total correction for that change is *fixed* — a conserved budget, apportioned across the cabinet's dimensions, the shares always summing back to the whole. That is, exactly, a **composition**: parts of a conserved total. The instrument was doing compositional analysis before the field of compositional data analysis had a name for it.

Twenty‑five years of building those instruments produced a way of *reading* any conserved mixture — not only sound, but any system where parts share a whole and the mix changes over time: a country's energy supply, a gut's bacteria, a market's sectors, the budget of the cosmos. The five papers are that reading, told in order. Each is a movement; together they are one instrument seen from first principle to full reach.

## Movement I — Exactness (P1)

*Tiling the simplex: an exact quaternion reading of compositional dynamics and lossless high‑dimensional reconstruction.*

The entry point, and the most surprising single fact in the whole body of work: a four‑part composition is mathematically identical to a unit quaternion — the same object that describes rotations in three dimensions — and a change in the composition is read as an exact rotation, to the last decimal a computer can hold. For larger compositions, the method tiles the problem into four‑part pieces and reconstructs the whole without loss. This is the bedrock: before anything is interpreted, it is read *exactly*. The value is set here — the work earns trust by starting from what is provably true, not from what is merely plausible.

## Movement II — Trust (P3)

*A deterministic, hash‑chained engine for compositional reading, with an honest guard layer.*

An exact method is only useful if the machine running it is trustworthy. P3 is the instrument itself: deterministic (the same input yields a bit‑identical output and a matching cryptographic fingerprint on any platform), and — the part that matters most — *honest about its own limits*. It holds instead of naming a leader when nothing has really moved; it flags when a reading is near the edge of what the data can resolve; it refuses, behind hard safety stops, to act when it should not. This is the system's central conviction made into code: an instrument powerful enough to be handed to a non‑expert must be one that *cannot* hand back a confident falsehood. The same idea behind responsible automation — capture the expert, build the guards that lift everyone else safely.

## Movement III — Motion (P4)

*Compositional kinematics: the mechanics of compositional trajectories.*

Most compositional analysis describes a snapshot. P4 lets the snapshot move. A mixture changing over time has a velocity, an acceleration, a momentum — an *arrow of intent*, the net direction the system is heading right now (a reading of motion, never a prediction). This is presented humbly: it is recognition and synthesis, the calculus the physical sciences already use, carried carefully into compositional geometry, citing the lineages it draws from. It extends the established field into dynamics without claiming to overturn any of it — the geometry stays the community's; the motion is the contribution.

## Movement IV — Character (P5)

*Compositional Character Space: a cross‑domain taxonomy of compositional dynamics.*

Here the instrument is turned on its own outputs. Read many systems, take each one's reading as itself a point, and a map appears: systems sort into a few recurring *characters* — directed, contested, churning, diffusive — and the sorting is coherent across completely unrelated fields. The most directed systems in a 107‑system survey were the cosmic microwave background, the world's energy transition, and a designed climate policy; the most churning were gut microbiomes, human conversation, and finely mixed rock chemistry. A market and a microbiome share a character. This movement also carries the clearest demonstration of the system's honesty: an early, clean‑looking result (a tight collapse to ~3 organizing axes from a small sample) was tested at scale and corrected to a milder ~4 — and that correction is published *as part of the result*, because a method that visibly corrects itself is more trustworthy than one that never appears to err.

## Movement V — Vigilance (P2)

*Deceptive drift: detecting concentration that hides behind quiet motion.*

The last movement is a guard. Some of the most consequential changes in a system are the quiet ones — a mixture tightening its concentration while its motion looks calm, the kind of drift that ordinary monitoring misses. P2 is a detector built specifically for that case. It closes the arc on the value that runs through everything: the instrument's job is not to reassure, it is to *reveal* — including, especially, what would otherwise stay hidden.

## What the system is trying to do

Read the five together and the intent is plain. This is an **instrument, not an oracle** — it reads what is there and leaves the meaning to the expert who knows the domain. It is built to **lift people safely**: the expertise is captured in the engine, and the guards let a newcomer use it without being able to extract a false certainty from it — the same shape as responsible automation and the AI transition, applied to one well‑understood corner of measurement. It **reveals rather than creates**: a clean reading, a held position when nothing moved, an honest correction at scale — none of it manufactured to impress, all of it to show the system as it is. And it is **honest by construction**: claims are tiered, the loop is kept open (the instrument observes; it does not silently decide), raw data is read but never absorbed into the claims, and a human remains the sole gate on every outward step. The work was done first and the papers written after — the labor was the knowing; the papers are the careful transcription of something already true and stress‑tested.

The throughline, in one line: **coherence** — the principle the founding loudspeaker was engineered around, the quantity the character map found organizes everything, and the discipline the whole body of work is held to.
