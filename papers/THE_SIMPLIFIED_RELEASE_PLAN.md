## The honest adoption argument (what "disadvantaged by blindness" really means)

The strong line — *adopt, or fall behind* — must be told honestly, or it is just fear.

- **What is measured (T1):** a reader who watches only **levels** misses what lives in the **ratios.** This is
  not rhetoric — it is demonstrated: the **deceptive/silent drift** (every alarm green while the mixture
  turned toward failure), the **see-or-remain-blind** cost finding, the locked-discriminant, even the Romance
  language clustering invisible to a level read. On **compositional data, the relational reader sees real,
  valuable events the absolute reader cannot.** That asymmetry is receipted.
- **The honest extrapolation (T2):** *where your data is a composition and the action lives in the
  proportions,* not adopting the relational view leaves real structure unseen — a genuine informational
  disadvantage, **there.** That is the truthful core of "disadvantaged by their blindness."
- **The fences (what we do NOT claim):** not a universal "adopt or perish"; not for non-compositional data
  (the friendly door — *composition-is-qualifier*); not coercion. We **reveal and invite**; we do not
  threaten. The advantage is *the seeing*, offered freely (the repo), and the human keeps the gate.

So the future this brings the right reader into is real: once you can see the ratios, the level-only view
looks like reading half the gauge. We make that visible, for free, and let them choose.
