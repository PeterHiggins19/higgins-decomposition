## Deterministic trust *for systems*

The "for systems" is the whole point. Human-to-human trust runs on reputation and review; **system-to-system
trust cannot** — a machine handing a composition to another machine needs trust it can *check*, in the loop,
without a person. The trust model supplies exactly that:

- **the receipt** — a deterministic fingerprint any system re-runs to confirm the reading bit-for-bit;
- **the locked invariant** — the guarantee that the reading is reproducible across the nuisance group, so two
  systems reading the same truth get the same answer;
- **the coherence certificate (Q)** — independent agreement, so trust rests on convergence, not on a single
  authority;
- **the gate** — the system withholds at low margin instead of manufacturing confidence, and **the human
  keeps the final breaker.**

Trust becomes a *computed quantity* that travels with the data — which is what lets large systems of systems
hand results to each other and have the trust mean something. That is the chain the feedback loop compounds
(`THE_FEEDBACK_CHAIN_IS_THE_GOAL.md`): science advances the physics, someone advances the data, and the trust
model makes each handoff verifiable so the compounding is real and not faked.
