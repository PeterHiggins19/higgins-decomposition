## The privacy line (read first)

This showcase contains **only public data and engine performance.** It deliberately contains:

- **No business names, no people, no private correspondence, no proposals, no deal terms.** Any real engagement (a winery, a partner, a person) is kept entirely off the public repository, in the private `Pipeline‑Projects/` engagement space, and is **not referenced here**.
- The principle: *they control their image, not us.* We provide the demonstrable capability on public data; the background, the brand, and the story are theirs to tell if and when they choose.
- This is how every relationship here is meant to work — **symbiotic**: the instrument expands a public‑facing capability in a direction that is beneficial to the partner, without exposing anything they have not chosen to make public. (For science collaborations whose work is already public — e.g. a published geoscientist — we may build on that *already‑public* face; for private businesses we expose nothing.)

If you are here from industry: this shows you what the engine would say about your sector's public data. What it would say about *your* data, and whether that is useful to you, is a private conversation you control.

## Why these two industries, these two countries

Both countries are mid‑transition in energy and both have a wine sector — two clean compositional stories (parts of a whole, tracked over time or across samples) where the engine's reading is immediately legible. The point is not the specific result; it is that **the same instrument reads both, deterministically, and now says honestly where its read is solid and where it isn't** — exactly the property a public‑facing showcase needs.

*The instrument reads. The expert decides. The partner keeps their image. Public data in, engine performance out — nothing private exposed.*
